The Forum has one long-term goal. In 2038, Genadendal turns 300. By then, we want every village in Ward 2 to be able to say:

- We work together to make a living.
- We are treated as equals.
- We are free to learn, believe, and celebrate in our own way.

We also want the gap between villages to close. A good result in one village doesn't count if another village is left behind.

A proposal now before the Forum, [[concepts/vorming|Vorming]], would add a fourth line: *We care for our land and water, and it shows in us and in the valley.* It has not yet been agreed.

## Three parts of life, three values

This site sorts the valley's work into three spheres of life. Each sphere has its own value:

| Sphere                       | Its value   | What it covers                        |
| ---------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| [[economic/index\|Economic]] | Cooperation | Farming, business, jobs, trade        |
| [[civic/index\|Civic]]       | Equality    | Rights, services, safety, land, care  |
| [[cultural/index\|Cultural]] | Freedom     | Schools, heritage, sport, arts, faith |

Each sphere works best when it respects its own value. Problems start when one sphere takes over another. For example, when money decides who gets land or water, or when politics decides what children learn or which events get support. Part of our goal is to keep each sphere functioning while aligned to its own value.

## How we plan to get there

We plan to build what we call a [BioConomy](https://bioconomy.earth) in the valley. In plain terms, it means running more of the local economy so that what the valley has stays in the valley: money spent at local shops and farms, skills in our young people, water in the ground, and healthy soil.

Shops, farms, and markets carry on as they do now. The BioConomy adds ways for neighbours and businesses to work together on things nobody pays for today, such as clean rivers, healthy veld, and passing on heritage. It uses tools like co-operatives and shared agreements between the people who depend on the same land and water.

### Beneath it: Vorming (proposed)

Tools and money only work when people are ready to carry what they create. [[concepts/vorming|Vorming]] is a proposal, not yet agreed, for the valley's own variation of Bildung, the tradition of lifelong formation. It is based on care for the valley and on ecological restoration at a large scale. Under Vorming, people grow in responsibility for the valley by working together on its land and water, and the state of its rivers and slopes shows that growth.

If the Forum adopts it, Vorming would sit beneath everything on this page:

- **Formation before funding.** Before a large project takes money, it would ask whether the people involved are ready to steward what the money builds.
- **Restoration as shared work.** Restoring a river brings people from every village to the same place. [[concepts/the-palmiet-principle|The Palmiet Principle]] is its first practice: every act of care for a river is seen and recorded, and nothing is punished.
- **Learning shared with the world.** The [[concepts/valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] would be built on Vorming, and would offer what the valley learns to people from elsewhere.
- **One question for every initiative.** Does it help the people who take part grow in responsibility for the valley?

### Our theory of change

We believe that:

1. When the valley can see what each initiative needs and offers (this is what [[register|The Register]] is for),
2. people and businesses in the valley can meet more of those needs from inside the valley,
3. so more money, work, and skills stay here,
4. and over time people work together more, villages get a fairer share, and people have more room to learn and create.

The Vorming proposal would add a step before the first: when people grow in responsibility for the valley through shared work on its land and water, they are ready to carry what the other steps build.

The yearly survey below will show whether this is true. If it's not working, the survey will tell us, and we will change our approach.

## How the five priorities fit

Each of the Forum's five [[priorities/index|priorities]] moves the valley towards the goal. Most touch more than one sphere.

| Priority | What it does | Spheres it helps most |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[priorities/tourism-development\|Tourism Development]] | Visitors spend money with valley businesses and guides, in every village | Economic, Cultural |
| [[priorities/education-skills\|Education and Skills]] | Young people learn skills the valley needs, so they can work and build businesses here | Cultural, Economic |
| [[priorities/agriculture-food-sovereignty\|Agriculture and Food Sovereignty]] | The valley grows more of its own food, and every household can get enough | Economic, Civic |
| [[priorities/conservation-environment\|Conservation and Environment]] | We look after the water, soil, and veld that everyone depends on | Civic, Economic |
| [[priorities/business-procurement\|Business Development and Local Procurement]] | Businesses and the municipality buy from valley suppliers first, and every supplier has a fair chance | Economic, Civic |

Under the Vorming proposal, each priority would also be judged by how it forms the people who carry it. Conservation work, for example, would restore the land and, through the same work, the people who restore it. Tourism businesses could adopt a stretch of river under [[concepts/the-palmiet-principle|The Palmiet Principle]], and schools could learn on it.

## How we will know: the yearly survey

The Forum plans to run a short survey once a year, starting in 2027. Young people from each village will be trained and paid to visit homes and ask the questions on a phone. The survey will take about 15 minutes, in Afrikaans or English.

We will ask the same questions every year, so we can see what changes. Results will be published on this site, village by village. The idea is set out in full on [[concepts/the-yearly-valley-survey|The Yearly Valley Survey]]: why the survey helps the valley speak with one voice, and what it could measure.

The survey is anonymous. We will not record names or addresses. Small hamlets will be grouped together in the results so that no household can be recognised. See the [[data-policy|Data Policy]].

### Example questions

Most questions can be answered with *Yes*, *Partly*, *No*, or *Don't know*.

**We work together (Economic)**

- In the past month, how much of your food came from growers or shops in the valley?
- In the past year, did you share, swap, or trade something with a neighbour?
- Is it easier or harder than last year to earn a living in the valley?

**We are treated as equals (Civic)**

- Does the municipality treat your village the same as other villages in the ward?
- Do you have a say in decisions that affect your village?
- Do you feel safe in your home and in your street?

**We are free (Cultural)**

- Can your children learn in their home language?
- Are you free to practise your faith and your traditions?
- If you wanted to start a club, a class, or a cultural event, could you?

**We care for our land and water (Vorming, proposed)**

- In the past year, did you help care for a river, a slope, a garden, or another shared place in the valley?
- Do you know where the water in your tap comes from?
- In the past year, did someone in the valley teach you a skill, or did you teach someone?

**Keeping each sphere committed to its own value**

- Does money decide things in the valley that should be decided fairly?
- Does politics affect who gets help or services?

**Your view**

- What one change would make life in the valley better for your family?
