**Date:** Saturday 14 November 2026

**Contact:** Michael Haupt, [phone], mh@michaelhaupt.com

## Purpose

Members of the Places Conversation, convened by Ranyaka, meet people already working in the valley. Together they hear what the valley has to offer and what it needs, and look for one or two things worth testing together. No decisions will be made on the day, and nothing will be put to the community for approval.

### The valley on one map

- The Greyton Governance Map and the Surveyor General land layer
- Farm 39, and Langgezocht (Farm 32), which protects the mission's water source
- About 11,194 ha of valley land that could retain water
- The Forum's five priorities: tourism development, education and skills, agriculture and food sovereignty, conservation and environment, business development and local procurement

### Offers and needs

Each participant answers two questions:

1. What does my work, group or organization already have that others in this room could use?
2. What do we need that we cannot provide ourselves?

Answers go onto a shared wall chart. Where an offer meets a need, the group notes it as a possible experiment for the next session.
