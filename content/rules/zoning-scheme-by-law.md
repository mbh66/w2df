Theewaterskloof Municipality's Zoning Scheme By-law is the land use scheme the [[rules/spatial-planning-and-land-use-management-act|Spatial Planning and Land Use Management Act]] requires: binding rules on what every piece of land in the municipality may be used for [IC]. It was published in the Provincial Gazette on 24 August 2020, and it applies to the entire municipal area [IC]. Each property has a zoning. The zoning says what the owner may do as of right (a primary use), what needs the municipality's consent (a consent use), and the limits on building, such as height and building lines [IC]. A revised scheme was published in draft in March 2025 and February 2026 [IC].

## What it does

- **It covers the whole municipality.** The scheme applies to the entire municipal area [IC]. A schedule converts zonings under the older schemes into the new zones [IC].
- **It sorts land into zones.** Each zone lists primary uses, consent uses, and development rules [IC]. Some examples that matter in the valley [IC]:

| Zone | Allowed as of right | Needs the municipality's consent (among others) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Residential Zone 1 | A dwelling house | A guest house, a house shop, a house tavern, a second dwelling larger than 60 m² |
| Agricultural Zone 1 | Agriculture | A farm shop, tourist facilities |

- **It has overlay zones.** Overlays add rules over the base zoning in a defined area. The scheme provides for a Subdivisional Area overlay, a Special Planning Area overlay, and an Urban Conservation overlay [IC].
- **Changes go through the planning by-law.** A rezoning, a consent use, or a departure is applied for under the [[rules/municipal-land-use-planning-by-law|Municipal Land Use Planning By-law]] [IC].

## What it means in the valley

**For guest houses and home businesses.** The municipality's spatial plan calls tourism Greyton's main economic sector [IC], and a guest house in a Single Residential zone needs the municipality's consent [IC]. So does a house shop or a house tavern [IC]. A resident in Greyton, Heuwelkroon, or Genadendal who wants to open one applies for consent, and neighbours are notified.

**For farms.** A farm shop or tourist facilities on agricultural land need consent [IC]. The farm stall, tasting room, or overnight huts that agri-tourism ideas for the valley propose would need this.

**For Greyton's character.** The municipality's spatial plan describes an urban conservation area covering Greyton's original layout of 1854 [IC]. The scheme's Urban Conservation overlay is the tool that would give such an area binding rules. Whether the overlay has been applied to Greyton, or to the heritage precincts the spatial plan identifies in Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Boschmanskloof, is not yet known [TBV]. Heritage protection also comes separately from the [[rules/national-heritage-resources-act|National Heritage Resources Act]].

**On Farm 39.** The scheme applies to the whole municipal area, which includes [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] [IC]. The scheme itself does not name Genadendal or the other mission settlements [IC]. The [[rules/western-cape-land-use-planning-act|Western Cape Land Use Planning Act]] requires land once governed by the Rural Areas Act to be given a zoning or a deemed zoning [IC]. What zoning the settlements and the Remainder carry today is a question for the municipality [TBV].

**The revision.** The municipality published drafts of a revised Zoning Scheme By-law in March 2025 and February 2026 [IC]. A by-law may only be passed after public comment [IC]. Whether the revision has been adopted is not yet known [TBV]. When it comes before the council, the Forum reads it as a chance for the valley's residents bodies and initiatives to check that the zones fit what the valley does.
