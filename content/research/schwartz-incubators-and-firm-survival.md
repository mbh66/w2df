*A Control Group Study of Incubators' Impact to Promote Firm Survival* is a journal article by M. Schwartz (2013), on whether firms sheltered in business incubators outlast similar firms. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].
