*Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena* is a reference article by J.A. Millard (1999), on Magdalena, in the Dictionary of African Christian Biography. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

## As cited on this site

- Millard, J.A. (1999). [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Dictionary of African Christian Biography. An account of Magdalena drawn from Schmidt's records, including the quarrel of 1739.
- Dictionary of African Christian Biography. [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Her baptism in 1742 and death on 3 January 1800.
- Millard, J.A. (1999). [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Dictionary of African Christian Biography. Schmidt's four best students, and Africo as the first convert baptised.
- Dictionary of African Christian Biography. [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Magdalena's baptism and her 48 years.
- Millard, J.A. (1999). [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Dictionary of African Christian Biography. His name as Janneke or Jantjie, his work for the mission, and why he was not baptised with his wife.
- Millard, J.A. (1999). [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Dictionary of African Christian Biography. Schmidt's four best students, the language of teaching, and the first baptisms.
- Millard, J.A. (1999). [Tikhuie, Vehettge Magdalena](https://dacb.org/stories/southafrica/tikhuie-vmagdalena/). Dictionary of African Christian Biography, reproduced from *Malihambe: Let the Word Spread* (Unisa Press). Her origins, husband, schooling, the quarrel of 1739, her baptism, the 48 years, the missionaries' account, and her death.
