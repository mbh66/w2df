*TRANCRAA and Communal Land Rights: Lessons from Namaqualand* is a policy brief by P. Wisborg and R. Rohde (2003), published by the Institute for Poverty, Land and Agrarian Studies (PLAAS) at the University of the Western Cape as Policy Brief 4, on TRANCRAA and communal land rights in Namaqualand.
