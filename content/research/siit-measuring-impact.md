*Measuring Impact* is a paper by the Impact Measurement Working Group of the Social Impact Investment Taskforce (2014), set up under the United Kingdom's presidency of the G8, on how investors and the organisations they fund can measure the social and environmental effects of their work. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

The BioConomy wiki explains the guidelines in full at [Impact Measurement Guidelines](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/impact-measurement-guidelines).

## As cited on this site

- Social Impact Investment Taskforce. [Measuring Impact](https://gsgii.org/reports/measuring-impact/) (September 2014), by the Impact Measurement Working Group. The guidelines for showing whether a programme is working.
