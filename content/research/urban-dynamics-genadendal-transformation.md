*Genadendal Transformation: Executive Summary and Recommendations* is a report by Urban Dynamics Western Cape, dated October 2010. The firm was appointed in 2002 as consultants on the transfer of Genadendal's land under TRANCRAA, and the report covers the transformation process and the consultants' recommendations. The register cites an archived copy from the Ward 2 Forum's website. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].
