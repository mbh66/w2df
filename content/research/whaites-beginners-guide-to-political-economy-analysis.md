*The Beginner's Guide to Political Economy Analysis* is a guide by A. Whaites (2017), published by the National School of Government International in the United Kingdom, on how practitioners can find out who wants what in a situation, and why. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

The BioConomy wiki explains the method in full at [Political Economy Analysis](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/political-economy-analysis).
