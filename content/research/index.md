This page lists the research the site draws on: theses, books, journal articles, studies, reports, and archival editions. Each work has a short page of its own, saying how the site cites it and which pages use it. If you want to add research to the site, check this list first. The work may already be here, or a page may already cite it.

The list covers research only. Laws, news reports, official web pages, and organisations' own websites are cited on the pages that use them, and are not listed here.

## How to read the list

- **Field** groups each work by subject: History; Land and tenure; Water and environment; Education and learning; Heritage and music; Economy and finance; Society and governance.
- **Kind** says what sort of work it is: a thesis, a book, a journal article, a report, and so on.
- **Read in full** shows "no" where the site cites a work it has not yet read in full. These are good places to start if you want to help. The *Not yet read* tab lists them.
