The *Sustainable Livelihoods Guidance Sheets* are a set of guidance sheets by the UK Department for International Development (1999) on the sustainable livelihoods approach, setting out a framework for how people make a living and what shapes their choices. This page records how the register cites them and which pages use them. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

The BioConomy wiki explains the framework in full at [Sustainable Livelihoods Framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/sustainable-livelihoods-framework).

## As cited on this site

- Department for International Development. [Sustainable Livelihoods Guidance Sheets, Section 2: Framework](https://eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/1999_sustainable_livelihoods_guidance_sheet_dfid.pdf) (April 1999). The framework for what households and villages hold.
