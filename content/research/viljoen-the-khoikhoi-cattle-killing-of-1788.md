*Making Sense of the Khoikhoi Cattle-Killing of 1788* is a journal article by R. Viljoen (1997), on the cattle-killing movement of 1788. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

## As cited on this site

- Viljoen, R. (1997). ["Making Sense of the Khoikhoi Cattle-Killing of 1788: An Episode of Millenarianism in Khoikhoi Society"](https://emandulo.apc.uct.ac.za/collection/FHYA%20Depot/Viljoen_Making_sense_of_the_Catte_Killing.pdf). *Kronos* 24, pages 62 to 76. The prophecy, its instructions, the number of followers, the trial and sentence, Paerl's marriage and work, and his turn to mission Christianity. Based on Cape Archives records.
