*Riviersonderend Complex Management Plan* is a report by CapeNature (2021), on the protected areas of the Riviersonderend mountains, their heritage, and the 2019 fire. This page records how the register cites it and which pages use it. It is part of the [[research/index|Research Register]].

- CapeNature. *Riviersonderend Complex Protected Area Management Plan 2021 to 2031*. The Greyton fire of December 2019.
