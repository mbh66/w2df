The Greyton MTB Club looks after the mountain-bike trails around Greyton, and publishes gravel routes for longer rides [MS]. Riders need a permit to use the trails [MS]. The club's youth programme is the [[greyton-mtb-juniors|Greyton MTB Juniors]] [MS].

In 2026 the Absa Cape Epic raced a stage on Greyton's trails, including Luislang, 2nd Bench, and Bakenskop. The local trail team worked with the race's trail builders to prepare them [IC].

## Needs
- Riders to buy permits [MS]

## Offers
- **Seven mountain-bike trails**, from 9 to 24 kilometres. They range from the easy Forest Loop, suitable for families with young children, to the more challenging Wreck and Luislang routes [MS].
- **Seven gravel routes**, from 23 to 70 kilometres, through farmland, orchards, and along the Sonderend River [MS]
- **Route maps.** A GPX file for every trail and route, free to download [MS]
- **Permits.** A year's membership costs R800 for a rider aged 16 or older. Visitors can buy a day permit (R75) or a weekend permit (R150) [MS].
- **A trailhead** where riders can buy permits and start their rides [MS]

## How to get involved
Buy a permit or download route maps from the [Greyton MTB website](https://www.greytonmtb.co.za/). For anything else, contact the moderators via [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].
