You can read this wiki in a language other than English, for example Afrikaans or isiXhosa.

## Choosing a language

1. Open any page of the wiki.
2. Find the box in the lower left corner with flags. If you don't see it, skip ahead to *"Getting the language box back."*
3. Pick your language from the list.

The page changes into your language.

## When you open another page

When you click a link to another page, the language box disappears. This is normal, and nothing is broken.

The wiki remembers the language you picked. Every page you open after that shows in your language, so you don't need the box again.

## Getting the language box back

If you want to change to a different language, or go back to English, refresh the page. The box comes back.

On a computer:

- Windows: press Ctrl + Shift + R
- Mac: press Cmd + Shift + R

On a phone or tablet:

- Tap the reload button in your browser (the round arrow), or
- Pull the page down from the top and let go.

Then pick a new language from the box. To read in English again, pick English from the list.

## A note about the translation

A computer does the translation, so some words or sentences may come out wrong. The English version is the official one. If something in your language is unclear or seems wrong, switch back to English or ask someone to check it with you.
