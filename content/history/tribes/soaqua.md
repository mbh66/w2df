The Soaqua were the [[history/tribes/san|San]] hunter-gatherers of the south-western Cape, including the Riviersonderend mountains [IC]. They are the first people of this valley whose name has come down to us [IC]. The name was given to them by the Khoekhoe herders, and the Dutch took it over in their records [MS]. The Soaqua were San, the same people, and some older sources that call them "ancestors of the San" are describing the same people in different words.

## The name

"Soaqua" (or "Sonqua") was the Khoekhoe name for the hunter-gatherers who lived among and around them [IC]. One museum explains it as meaning "people different from ourselves", a name herders used for people they saw as lower in status [MS]. It was not the name the Soaqua used for themselves, which is not recorded.

## Life in the valley

The Soaqua lived in the Cape for thousands of years, hunting game and gathering plants [IC]. Their rock paintings survive in the Overberg, most of them found on the drier northern side of the Riviersonderend mountain [IC]. Archaeologists have noted signs of the Soaqua in the mountains north of Genadendal [IC].

About 2,000 years ago, [[history/tribes/khoekhoe|Khoekhoe]] herders arrived with sheep and cattle [IC]. The herders took the best grazing, and the Soaqua moved higher into the mountains. There they lived on plants and on small animals such as tortoises and dassies [IC].

Fourteen cave sites in the Riviersonderend mountains are on a heritage list [TBV]. They have not been formally surveyed or dated, so much of the Soaqua story in this valley is still in the ground [TBV].

## After the colony came

As colonists spread across the Cape in the 1700s, the San, including the Soaqua, lost their land and game, and many were killed on the colonial frontier [IC]. See [[history/tribes/san|San]]. Some Soaqua families were drawn into the Khoekhoe and colonial world as herders and farm workers, and their descendants live on in the Western Cape [TBV].

## What is still open

- What the Soaqua called themselves.
- The age of the rock art and the cave sites in the Riviersonderend mountains.
- Whether any families in the valley trace their descent to the Soaqua.
