The Hessequa were a [[history/tribes/khoekhoe|Khoekhoe]] people of the southern Cape [IC]. Historians place their main grazing lands east of the Breede River, beyond the [[history/tribes/chainoqua|Chainoqua]] [IC]. The Hessequa Municipality around Riversdale, which stretches from the Breede River to the Gourits River, is named after them [IC]. Greyton's histories say the Hessequa grazed the valley of the Gobos River, where Greyton now stands, and the Moravian Church in South Africa says [[history/people/magdalena|Magdalena]] was Hessequa [MS]. How far west Hessequa families lived is one of the open questions of the valley's early history.

## The Hessequa and the Dutch

The Hessequa were among the richest cattle-keeping peoples the Dutch met in the Cape [TBV]. From 1662 to 1713, the Hessequa and the Chainoqua were the largest suppliers of cattle and sheep to the colony [IC]. From 1679 to 1693 the Chainoqua leader [[history/people/dorha|Dorha]] was the company's go-between with both peoples [IC].

As colonists moved east, the company set up a magistracy at Swellendam in 1745, in the area where Hessequa and Chainoqua land met [TBV]. Smallpox killed many Khoekhoe in 1713 and 1755 [IC]. By the end of the 1700s most Hessequa had lost their land and herds [TBV].

## In the valley

Greyton's histories say the Hessequa (or Hassequas) kept thousands of cattle and sheep in kraals along the Gobos River, and named the river after an ancestral chief [MS]. They name [[history/people/stoffel-koekson|Stoffel Koekson]] as a Hessequa leader who settled at Boschmanskloof in the late 1600s [MS]. One history also names the Attaqua, a people further east, among the area's first inhabitants [TBV].

The Moravian Church in South Africa says that Vehettge Tikkuie, baptised [[history/people/magdalena|Magdalena]] in 1742, was Hessequa, and that she married a Chainoqua man, [[history/people/janneke-tikkuie|Janneke Tikkuie]] [MS].

These accounts do not sit easily with the historians' map, which puts this valley in Chainoqua land [IC]. The Hessequa may have grazed further west than the historians' map shows, individual Hessequa families may have lived among the Chainoqua, or later sources may have used the name loosely [TBV].

## Today

The name lives on in the Hessequa Municipality [IC]. People of Hessequa descent have begun work to restore and revive Hessequa culture [MS].

## What is still open

- What "Hessequa" means, and how the name was said.
- Whether Hessequa families lived in the Sonderend valley, and when.
- Whether Koekson and Magdalena were Hessequa.
