Kupido and Willem were two Khoekhoe men who studied with [[history/people/georg-schmidt|Georg Schmidt]] at Baviaanskloof from 1738 [MS]. With [[history/people/africo|Africo]] and [[history/people/magdalena|Vehettge (Magdalena)]], they were Schmidt's four best students [MS]. In 1742 both were baptised: Willem with the name Josua, and Kupido with the name Jonas [MS]. They were among the first five Christians in the valley [MS]. This page covers the two together, because the records say so little about each.

## What is known

Both men were among the Khoekhoe who came to Schmidt's school, first near the Sonderend River in 1737 and then at Baviaanskloof from 1738 [MS]. Schmidt taught in Dutch, because he could not master the clicks of the Khoekhoe language [MS]. Kupido and Willem learned to read and helped in the life of the station [MS].

When Schmidt's authority to baptise arrived in 1742, Willem was the first he baptised, on 2 April 1742 [MS]. The historian Mansell Upham, working from Schmidt's records, describes Africo as Willem's brother-in-law [MS]. The sources give the baptismal names of the first five as Josua (Willem), Christian (Africo), Jonas (Kupido), [[history/people/christina|Christina]], and Magdalena (Vehettge) [MS]. On 4 September 1742 the VOC's Council of Policy banned Schmidt from baptising any more Khoekhoe [TBV]. Some sources give Kupido's name as Kybbodo. It is likely, but not certain, that these are the same man [TBV].

Two years later Schmidt was forced to leave the Cape [IC]. What happened to Kupido and Willem after that is not recorded. By 1792 both had died [MS].

## Why they matter

The Dutch Reformed ministers at the Cape objected to Schmidt's baptisms, and this was one reason he had to leave [IC]. Kupido and Willem were among the people at the centre of that dispute. Their baptism, and the dispute that followed, marked the start of the Christian community that became Genadendal. Their names show how little of the Khoekhoe side of the story was recorded.

## What is still open

- Their Khoekhoe names. "Kupido" and "Willem" are Dutch names, probably given by colonists [TBV].
- Whether Kupido and Kybbodo are the same person.
- What became of them after 1744, and whether families in the valley descend from them.
