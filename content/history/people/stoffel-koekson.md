Stoffel Koekson was a Khoekhoe leader in the area where Greyton now stands [TBV]. Greyton's local histories say he traded cattle with the Dutch in the late 1600s, grew wealthy, and moved his people to [[villages/boschmanskloof|Boschmanskloof]], where they built houses of mud brick [TBV]. The foundations of those houses are said to lie under the old houses of Boschmanskloof today [TBV]. If the story is right, Boschmanskloof is one of the oldest settled places in the valley.

## His story as Greyton tells it

Greyton's histories say that before the Dutch came, the plains and wooded ravines of the area were home to a Khoekhoe people they call the Hessequa (or Hassequas) [TBV]. These histories say the people named the Gobos River after an ancestral chief [TBV].

In the late 1600s, the VOC sent "Ensign Schriver" from the Castle of Good Hope to barter cattle with their headman, Captain Stoffel Koekson [TBV]. Koekson prospered, and took his people to live in Boschmanskloof, where he built them proper houses of mud brick [TBV].

In 1793 the land said to have been Koekson's was granted by the colony to a young Dutchman, [[history/people/marthinus-theunissen|Marthinus Theunissen]], who farmed it as Weltevreden [IC]. Sixty years later, [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] laid out the village of Greyton on Weltevreden [IC].

## Checking the story

Parts of the story fit the colonial record. Isaq Schrijver was made an ensign in the VOC in 1687, and in 1689 he led a large cattle-trading expedition east through the Overberg [IC]. He may be the "Ensign Schriver" of the Greyton story [TBV].

Other parts do not yet fit. Historians place the Hessequa mainly east of the Breede River, and the Chainoqua, led earlier by [[history/people/soeswa|Soeswa]], on the Greyton side [IC]. Koekson may have led a Hessequa group living further west than usual, or a Chainoqua group that Greyton's histories name differently [TBV]. "Stoffel" is a Dutch name, short for Christoffel, so it was probably given to him by colonists [TBV]. His Khoekhoe name is not known.

## Links to other figures

- [[history/people/soeswa|Soeswa]] led the Chainoqua, whose land took in the valley, a generation before Koekson.
- [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] founded Greyton on land once held by Koekson's people. Vigne first settled on a farm at Boschmanskloof in 1846 [TBV].

## What is still open

- Whether the VOC journals of the 1680s and 1690s name Koekson, and how they spell his name.
- Whether he was Hessequa or Chainoqua.
- When and how his people lost the land that became Weltevreden.
- Whether the foundations at Boschmanskloof are his people's houses. A heritage survey could answer this.
- What Boschmanskloof families remember of him.
