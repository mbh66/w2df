This section holds one page for each person who has passed on and whose life shaped the valley. Some pages cover two or more people who acted together, where the records say too little to support a page for each. Every page in the section is listed below. For the same pages with each person's years and a line on who they were, see [[history/index|Valley History]].

## Who has a page here

- The valley's Khoekhoe and San figures are given the same attention as its missionaries and settlers.
- A page is only for someone who has passed on. A page about a person who died in living memory is published only after the moderators agree and the family has had the chance to respond.
- Living researchers whose work helps this wiki are recognised on [[contributors/index|Research Contributors]].

## How these pages are written

- Every fact carries a tag that shows where it comes from, and each page lists its sources.
- Where sources disagree on a date or an event, the page gives each account and says which source gives which.
- Much of what is known about these people comes from one side, often a missionary's own diary or the mission's records. The pages say so where it matters.
- The pages describe what people did and said. They do not guess at a person's thoughts or feelings beyond what the sources record.
