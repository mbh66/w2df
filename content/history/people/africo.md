Africo was the leader of a Khoekhoe clan living near the Sonderend River in 1737 [MS]. When Georg Schmidt arrived in the area that year, he pitched his tent at Africo's kraal [MS]. Africo became one of Schmidt's best students, and in 1742 he was among the first people Schmidt baptised, with the name Christian [MS]. The meeting of 1738 that the valley will mark in 2038 began, in practice, as a welcome at his kraal.

## His life

In 1737 Africo led a clan whose kraal stood near a Dutch East India Company cattle post called Soetmelksvlei, roughly halfway between present-day Genadendal and Riviersonderend [MS]. The company had expanded its post there in 1727, to farm timber and cattle [IC]. Africo's people lived beside the colony's frontier and its demand for cattle.

He owned a hartebeeshuis, a house of reeds, mud, and thatch [MS]. When Schmidt arrived later in 1737, he pitched his tent at Africo's kraal and began teaching there [MS]. In 1738 Schmidt and eighteen followers moved to Sergeants River, the place later called Baviaanskloof and now Genadendal [MS]. The sources do not say whether Africo's whole clan moved with him.

Africo learned Dutch and helped Schmidt in his first months [MS]. He was one of Schmidt's four best students, with Kupido, Willem, and [[history/people/magdalena|Vehettge (Magdalena)]] [MS]. In December 1739 Schmidt recorded Africo adding a clay wall to a matjieshuis, a house of reed mats [MS].

In 1742 Schmidt baptised him with the name Christian [MS]. The historian Mansell Upham, working from Schmidt's records, describes Africo as Willem's brother-in-law [MS]. Some accounts say he was the first person Schmidt baptised, and others say Willem was first, on 2 April 1742 [MS]. The sources name five people baptised in that year: Christian (Africo), Josua (Willem), Jonas (Kybbodo or Kupido), Christina, and Magdalena (Vehettge) [MS].

Nothing is known of his later life. He had died by the time the missionaries returned in 1792 [MS].

## What is still open

- His Khoekhoe name. "Africo" may be a name given by colonists [TBV].
- Which Khoekhoe group his clan belonged to.
- Whether he moved to Baviaanskloof in 1738, and where he lived after Schmidt left in 1744.
- When he died, and whether families in the valley descend from him.
