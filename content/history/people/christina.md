Christina was one of the five Khoekhoe people [[history/people/georg-schmidt|Georg Schmidt]] baptised at Baviaanskloof in 1742 [MS]. With [[history/people/magdalena|Magdalena]], she was one of the first two women baptised in the valley [MS]. She is the least known of the five. The records give only her baptismal name and say that she was the sister of a man called Moses [MS]. Her page exists so that her place among the first Christians of Genadendal is not forgotten, and so that anyone who knows more can add to it.

## What is known

In 1742 Schmidt received a letter from Herrnhut giving him authority to baptise [IC]. He baptised Willem on 2 April 1742, and then Africo, Kupido, and Vehettge, who took the name Magdalena [MS]. The fifth person baptised that year was given the name Christina [MS]. The historian Mansell Upham, working from Schmidt's records, describes her as the sister of Moses [MS]. Who Moses was is not recorded in the sources found so far.

Her Khoekhoe name is not known.

On 4 September 1742 the VOC's Council of Policy at the Cape ruled that Schmidt was not qualified to baptise, and banned him from baptising any more Khoekhoe until further notice [TBV]. Christina was therefore among the last people Schmidt baptised at the Cape. He left in 1744 [IC].

## What is still open

- Her Khoekhoe name.
- Who her brother Moses was, and whether he was also one of Schmidt's students.
- Whether she stayed with Magdalena's small congregation after 1744.
- When she died, and whether families in the valley descend from her.
- The exact date of her baptism.
