The Waterberg Development Initiative (WDI) is a volunteer group in the Waterberg District of Limpopo [MS]. It works across the district's five local municipalities [MS]. It grew out of a meeting called by Kumba Iron Ore with the Marakele National Park manager, mine representatives and a tourism representative [MS]. Its directors say they started work on it in 2019 [MS]. It runs through a non-profit company whose directors also lead Waterberg Tourism [MS]. It says it identifies needs, facilitates and encourages co-operation, and it lists its projects in eight categories [MS]. Few of the results it reports have been checked by sources that do not depend on it. Henk Swanepoel, founder director of [[organisations/the-shift|The Shift]], named it to the Forum's convenor on 29 October 2026 [MS].

## What it does

WDI says its role is "to identify opportunities and needs in the Waterberg, to investigate them, to facilitate, to promote co-operation, to monitor and to encourage" [MS]. It says "the local authorities have neither the capacity nor the funding to fulfil the function of service delivery in the Waterberg" [MS]. It sorts its projects into eight categories: Job Creation, Security, Social Welfare, Business/Social, Skills Development, Infrastructure, Sport & Education, and Government [MS].

WDI uses different words for its part in different projects [MS]. It says it "initiated" some, "facilitated" or "assisted" others, and is "supporting" or "encouraging" more. This page keeps its own word for each project.

## How it started and how it is run

- **The first meeting.** Kumba Iron Ore, whose Thabazimbi mine was "due to go through mine closure", invited the Marakele National Park manager, mine representatives and Jacques Blaauw "as a representative of the tourism sector" [MS]. They decided "we couldn't wait for local authorities to develop the local economy; we needed to start doing it ourselves" [MS]. The date of the meeting was not found.
- **2019.** Dr Syd Catton put "the case for tourism" at the District Development Model conference in Lephalale [MS]. The District Development Model is a national plan for all spheres of government to plan and budget together, district by district. The Waterberg was one of its three pilot sites, and the model was launched there on 26 November 2019 [IC].
- **2019.** Waterberg Tourism was established, according to its founder Ken Maude [MS]. His director profile says he has been "establishing and developing Waterberg Development Initiative" since 2019 [MS].
- **August 2020.** The Waterberg Wild Dog Initiative started as a project "underneath the Waterberg Development Initiative NPC" [MS]. It became its own non-profit company in September 2022 [MS].
- **2 February 2023.** A Waterberg Landscape Alliance directory calls WDI "Waterberg Development Initiative NPC (formerly Waterberg Tourism NPC), a registered non-profit company and public benefit organization" [TBV]. No registration number was found in any public source.
- **Directors.** Waterberg Tourism's directors page, read on 1 November 2026, lists Daniel Ngwepe (patron), Desirée Sehlapelo, Jacques Blaauw, Dr Syd Catton (financial director of WDI), John Mahlangu, Zita Dube and Nkhangweleni Siliga [MS]. Earlier versions of the page also listed Ken Maud and Sean Hensman [MS].
- **Shared names.** WDI's "Support us here" link goes to Waterberg Tourism [MS]. Waterberg Tourism's 2023 project list is headed "Some of the projects Waterberg Development and Waterberg Tourism have been involved in" [MS]. Our inference: one company sits behind all three names, with Waterberg Tourism as its tourism side and Waterberg Community as its community side.
- **Volunteers.** Jacques Blaauw said: "As volunteers, our main aim is to ensure people know about each other and start talking about what the Waterberg is really about" [MS]. The number of volunteers was not found.
- **Money.** Jacques Blaauw said: "Unfortunately, funding is another big challenge for us" [MS]. He asks mining companies to put part of their social and labour plan and CSI budgets into tourism [MS]. A social and labour plan is the set of promises a mine must make to its workers and nearby communities to get its mining right. Waterberg Tourism has members, a membership fee and sponsors [MS]. No amounts were found.
- **Decisions.** How often WDI meets, who may join its table and how it chooses projects were not found.

## What it has done

Projects are grouped under WDI's own eight categories. Where a project is only named on WDI's site, the page says so.

### Job Creation

- WDI lists farmers' and night markets in Vaalwater, Bela-Bela and Modimolle [MS]. It says the Bela-Bela Night Market had "more than 80 vendors" in 2023 [MS].
- In March 2020 Vaalwater residents complained about a recycling site on the main road [MS]. WDI says it helped the owner find a new property, and that "more than three thousand people are earning money" from recycling (2023) [MS].
- WDI says it set up tourism information centres in Vaalwater, Thabazimbi and Bela-Bela [MS].

### Security

- Jacques Blaauw credits a network of road cameras to "various stakeholders' conservation efforts" [MS].
- WDI says it is "supporting the various significant security initiatives taking place across the Waterberg" [MS]. The security groups it lists are only named on its site.

### Social Welfare

- Bela-Bela private hospital: WDI "is facilitating the process of reopening it" (2024) [MS].
- Modimolle hospital: WDI says it "initiated and helped secure a permit and license" [MS].
- Hope for the Blind eye hospital: WDI says it "assisted in obtaining permits" [MS].
- Phodi clinic: only named on WDI's site.
- No outcome was found for any of these.

### Business/Social

- Friends of the Bushveld brings local business people together with tourism operators. Nine meetings had been held in Bela-Bela by 2024 [MS].
- The chambers of business and other bodies listed under this category are only named on WDI's site.

### Skills Development

- Ken Maude wrote that Waterberg Tourism had run "over 40 courses" at a new community centre in Vaalwater (March 2022) [MS]. The 2023 project list says "more than 50" [MS].
- He wrote that "four small business startups are now up and running in Vaalwater" (2022) [MS]. They are not named.
- WDI says it was Afrika Tikkun's "Ambassador" in the Waterberg, and that 15 early childhood centres with 560 children were helped (2023) [MS]. No Afrika Tikkun source naming the Waterberg was found.
- The Air BnB Academy is only named on WDI's site.

### Infrastructure

- WDI says it raised concerns about large trucks on its roads and delays for tourists on the M1 [MS]. No SANRAL source naming WDI was found.
- It says it helped set up a Greater Vaalwater roads committee and ran more than five clean-ups in Vaalwater [MS].
- The Rail Runner is only named on WDI's site.

### Sport & Education

- Minister of Justice Ronald Lamola presented 150 mountain bikes on 26 February [MS]. The year was not found. The minister held a charity mountain bike event in November 2022 to raise money for bikes for school children, so February 2023 is likely [TBV].
- Dr Catton says WDI created new mountain bike trails in Marakele National Park [MS].
- The park hosts the Powerade Marakele Marathon each year [MS]. WDI's role in it is not described.
- WDI says it "initiated and encouraged" the Waterberg Wild Dog Initiative [MS].
- WDI lists the Waterberg Biosphere expansion, the Conservation Forum, the Environmental Education Forum and the Lapalala Wilderness School [MS]. Its role in each is not described.

### Government

- WDI says it works with the Public Private Growth Initiative for "closer relations with the public sector" [MS].
- Dr Catton sits on tourism and local economic development committees in Bela-Bela and Modimolle, and on the Modimolle Small Town Regeneration committee [MS].

## Working with government

- No agreement or memorandum of understanding was found between WDI and any municipality, the Limpopo Department of Economic Development, Environment and Tourism, the Limpopo Tourism Agency, SANParks, CoGTA or SANRAL.
- When CoGTA launched the Waterberg District Development Model Hub in September 2020, its statement named "business through the Public-Private Growth Initiatives" as a partner [IC]. It did not name WDI.
- At an expert mission run by Connective Cities with SALGA and the Waterberg District Municipality on 8 to 12 June 2026, "the Waterberg Development Initiative (WDI) presented its vision for a coordinated regional development model" [IC].
- The parts of the district's 2026/27 Integrated Development Plan that were read do not name WDI [TBV]. The five local plans were not checked.
- No public statement by any government body about WDI was found.

The official record for the district's municipalities:

- The Waterberg District Municipality received a clean audit for 2023/24 [IC].
- Modimolle-Mookgophong received a qualified audit with findings for 2023/24 [IC]. Thabazimbi received a disclaimer of opinion for 2023/24 [IC].
- In August 2024 the Limpopo Executive Council decided to dissolve the Thabazimbi council under section 139(1)(c) of the Constitution, and the National Council of Provinces approved this on 3 October 2024 [IC].

## How its work reaches the valley

No public record of contact between WDI and Ward 2, Theewaterskloof or the Overberg was found. No public source links WDI to Henk Swanepoel, The Shift or [[organisations/meritus-international|Meritus International]].
