At that meeting, anyone can question it, change it, or reject it.

## The goal

> Development in Ward 2 has stalled. Drawing on the valley's history and on its conversations with residents and organisations, the Forum would propose what must happen first for each party, and ask each one to correct it.

## Why the valley needs it

[[challenges|Challenges: The Record and the Present State]] sets out where the valley's challenges came from and where they stand in 2026. Much of what it records has remained stuck for years:

- The Remainder of [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] is still held in trust by the national Minister, 28 years after TRANCRAA came into force, and no receiving body has been recognised [MS].
- The municipality's spatial plan says it "is not allowed to invest in infrastructure, where the Municipality is not the owner of the land" [IC].
- Electrification for [[villages/madiba-park|Madiba Park]] was budgeted at R1.6 million and cut in February 2023 [IC]. The R10 million for building homes on [[villages/erf-595-greyton|Erf 595]] was removed in February 2026 [IC].
- Genadendal's share of the municipality's debtors rose from 23.0% in October 2025 to 23.7% in May 2026, and it currently stands at R108m [IC].

The record shows what happened. Read together, it also points to what has to change before each matter can move. Nobody has yet set that reading out in one place, where every party can see it and test it.

## The idea

The Forum would take on a new role: proposing, for each party, what appears necessary before progress can be made. Its proposals draw on the historical record on the Challenges page and on conversations the Forum's convenors have held with people and organisations in the valley [MS]. Each proposal is the Forum's reading, and the party it concerns may reject it.

The Forum would send each party its row, in writing and in the language the party prefers, with two questions:

> **Does this match your reading? What would you change?**

Each response would be recorded in the party's own words, dated and tagged [MS], and the party would approve the wording before it is published. A disagreement stays on the page beside the proposal it answers. Where a response shows a proposal to be wrong, the Forum would revise the proposal and note the change. Until a party responds, its cell would read "Not yet asked", or "Sent on (date), no reply yet".

When most parties have responded, the Forum would read the responses together and note where two or more parties agree on what must happen first. Those findings would go into the second research run on the Challenges page.

## Who would benefit

Every party would see the Forum's reading of every row, and could answer its own. The Forum's champions would know which conditions stand in the way of their priority before they start work on it.

## The money

Nothing has been costed. The work would take moderators' time, translation, and meetings in the villages where no single body speaks for residents.

## Who would decide

The Forum would decide what it proposes in each row, who is asked, and who speaks for groups that have no single body. Because the table deals with Farm 39 land, the Transformasie Komitee would review the Farm 39 rows first. Each party decides its own response and approves how it is recorded.

## How it fits the Forum's goal

The Forum agreed at its [[civic/governance/minutes/2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|first meeting]] that affected communities should be involved from the start [MS]. This idea would put the Forum's reading in front of them before any project is planned on it. It serves [[long-term-goal|Our Long-Term Goal]] and all five [[priorities/index|Forum Priorities]], since each priority meets the conditions set out in the table.

## What is still open

1. **Who speaks for the descendants of the first peoples.** For the Forum to agree.
2. **Who speaks for Madiba Park residents and the Heuwelkroon households,** where no single body holds a mandate. One route would be a meeting in each place, with the Forum's proposal read out and the responses read back before they are recorded. Another would be a question in [[concepts/the-yearly-valley-survey|The Yearly Valley Survey]].
3. **The Ward 2 councillor.** Local government elections are set for 4 November 2026 [IC]. Whether the councillor's office gets a row, and when to ask, is open.
4. **Other parties.** Commercial farmers and landowners in the valley, and the [[organisations/breede-olifants-catchment-management-agency|Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency]] on water rights, may need rows.
5. **How a proposal is agreed.** Whether the convenors or a Forum meeting agrees the wording of each row before it is sent.
6. **Responses given in confidence.** What the Forum does when a party responds but asks that its response not be published.
7. **Each named party's view of this idea.** None has been asked.

## What happens next

1. The page goes to the Transformasie Komitee in writing, with the Farm 39 rows marked for its review.
2. It is tabled at a Forum meeting once the Komitee responds or 21 days after it was sent, whichever comes first. The Forum agrees the proposal in each row, who is asked, and who speaks for each group.
3. The moderators send each party its row, with the right-of-reply contacts already planned for the Challenges page.
4. Each response, once the party has approved its wording, is added beside the proposal with the date.
5. When most parties have responded, the Forum revises its proposals, and its findings go into the second research run.
