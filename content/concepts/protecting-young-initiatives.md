## The goal

> Every young initiative in the valley knows what protects it, who gives that protection, on what terms, and the date it will stand in the open.

## Why the valley needs it

[[guiding-ethos|Our Guiding Ethos]] asks of every initiative: if it's young, is it protected while it grows, and does it know when it will stand in the open? [MS] It also lists, as an open question, when protection should end and who decides [MS].

Several of the valley's ideas are young. Graduates of the October 2026 small business course now meet weekly to plan a co-operative and keep their own record [MS]. The [[concepts/valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Learning Campus]] would put "formation before funding" [MS]. [[concepts/the-palmiet-principle|The Palmiet Principle]] proposes a shared pool for each stretch of river [MS]. The [[political-economy-of-the-valley|Political Economy Analysis]] plans conversations across the valley in January and February 2027 [MS].

The Shift told the DevForum that capital speeds things up, and that where people aren't ready it can also speed up dependency [MS]. The DevForum has kept the idea that young initiatives need protection, and dropped any steering out of view: it decides at its meetings and records what happens between them on [[civic/governance/between-meetings/index|Between Meetings]] [MS].

## What the research says

Researchers who study how new practices grow call the shelter a young initiative needs *protective space* (see [[research/smith-raven-what-is-protective-space|What Is Protective Space?]]). Good protection holds back pressure while people learn and build networks, and it gets the initiative ready to stand on its own [IC]. The BioConomy wiki's brief, [Protective Space for Young Initiatives](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/research/protective-space-for-young-initiatives), sets out what the research shows:

- Most of the evidence comes from single cases of initiatives that lasted [IC].
- A survey of 276 Transition groups in 23 countries found that the less successful ones tended to underestimate their surroundings and the material resources they needed [IC].
- Ten years of records from five German business incubators found that sheltered firms didn't outlast similar firms that weren't sheltered, and that leaving the incubator was a dangerous time [IC].
- Protection can be captured by the people who give it, or can go on sheltering work that isn't improving [IC].
- Protection that was decided and recorded in public is well documented. The research has no finding that protection must be hidden to work [IC].

## The idea

For each young initiative, the DevForum would keep a short **protection record**, agreed at a DevForum meeting and published with the initiative's page:

| Question | What the record says |
| --- | --- |
| What is protected? | The initiative, and why it needs protection now |
| From what? | The pressure it isn't ready for: prices, rules, scrutiny, or a funder's measures |
| How? | Money, a buyer, a place, time, a network, a shared story, or keeping a low profile for now |
| Who gives it? | Each person or organisation, by name, and what they have said they will give |
| How is it watched? | How the group checks its own learning, without scoring people |
| When does it open up? | The review date, and what happens on that date |

## How it would apply

- **The Agri-Heritage Co-operative.** It already has weekly meetings, community mentors and its own record [MS]. Missing: an honest early look at land, water and costs, since the land is part of the unresolved [[rules/trancraa|TRANCRAA]] process [MS], and someone who brings lessons in from other co-operatives. A first opening point could be testing one product with outside buyers on an agreed date.
- **The Learning Campus and Vorming.** "Formation before funding" is already a kind of time-based protection [MS]. Missing: a review date for each part of the campus, and a way of tracking learning that doesn't pay or rank people per act. The first group of learners whose project work is checked by Umalusi would be a natural opening point.
- **The river work.** River logs, seasonal photographs and the yearly river walk let people see the work without scoring it [MS]. Missing: a longer time frame. Restoration that lasted elsewhere in Africa grew from long apprenticeships and secure local rights [MS].
- **The stretch pools.** Rewards for people are recognition only, and a sponsor gets no larger say for a larger contribution [MS]. Missing: a rule for what happens if a sponsor leaves, and a review date. The first money into a pool would be the moment to record its terms at a DevForum meeting.
- **The Political Economy Analysis.** Asking people questions can change what they say. [[concepts/what-must-happen-first|What Must Happen First]] already shows an open way to handle this: each party gets its row and its reply stays on the page [MS]. The analysis could also say, in its terms, which young initiatives it won't measure yet.

## Who would benefit

The people running young initiatives, who would know where they stand. The valley, which could see who protects what and why.

## Who would pay

Keeping the record costs very little. The protection itself would come from whoever offers it, as each record says.

## Who would decide

The DevForum, at its meetings, with the people running each initiative. Anything on Farm 39 land goes to the Transformasie Komitee first.

## How it fits the DevForum's goal

It answers the fourth question in [[guiding-ethos|Our Guiding Ethos]] and the open question on when protection should end. It works alongside [[concepts/vorming|Vorming]] and the [[long-term-goal|long-term goal]], and it serves four of the five [[priorities/index|priorities]].

## What is still open

- Who keeps the protection records, and where they're published.
- Whether each initiative wants a record, and who speaks for it.
- What happens when an initiative isn't ready on its review date.
- No study of community initiatives has tested whether a fixed opening date helps. The valley would be learning as it goes.

## What happens next

1. The idea goes to the Transformasie Komitee in writing.
2. The groups behind each initiative named above are asked whether they want a protection record, and what it should say.
3. The idea is tabled at a DevForum meeting.
4. The first records are drafted with the groups that want them.
