The Greyton Post is an independent community newspaper for Greyton and the Overberg [MS]. It comes out every two months in print and as a free digital edition, and its website carries news, events, and community notices [MS]. It is locally owned and written, and funded by advertising and donations from readers [MS].

In 2026 The Greyton Post was a national finalist in the mobile photography category of the AVBOB Forum of Community Journalists Awards of Excellence [MS].

## Needs
- Donations from readers, to keep the paper free [MS]
- Advertisers for the newspaper, the website directory, and the village map [MS]
- Local makers of art, ceramics, jewellery, textiles, homeware, and food, to sell through The Greyton Collective [MS]

## Offers
- **The newspaper.** Free every two months, with past issues kept online as digital editions [MS]
- **What's on.** A calendar of events in Greyton and the wider Overberg [MS]
- **Business directory.** Listings for local businesses, each with its own page. A listing costs R550 until January 2027 [MS].
- **Village map.** A numbered map of restaurants, shops, accommodation, and services in Greyton, shown on the noticeboard and as a PDF. A business can add its location for R300 [MS].
- **Community noticeboard.** A physical noticeboard at the Greyt Oak Centre for local news and events [MS]
- **The Greyton Collective.** A shop in the Oak & Vigne Courtyard selling original products made in Greyton and the Overberg. It supports makers with promotion, product photography, business advice, and Meet the Maker events [MS].
- **Emergency numbers.** A list of local emergency and service numbers on its website [MS]
