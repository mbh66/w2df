The Greyton Conservation Society (GCS) is a member-based non-profit and public benefit organisation with its own constitution [MS]. Since 1979 it has worked to protect Greyton's plants and wildlife, the natural landscape around the village, and the village's character [MS]. It supports Theewaterskloof Municipality in managing the Greyton Nature Reserve and the commonage around the village [MS].

The Greyton Nature Reserve was established on 13 May 1977 and covers about 2,200 hectares, up to the peaks of Uitkyk, Wa en Osse, and Perdekop [MS]. CapeNature's stewardship panel has approved a proposed extension of the reserve, but further steps are needed before it is final [MS].

The society is funded by the village market, monthly contributions from residents, membership fees, donations from visitors, and donors [MS]. The Sophia Foundation matches community donations to the Greyton Nature Programme Rand for Rand, up to R500,000 a year [MS].

## Needs
- Members. Supporter membership costs R100 a year. Households are asked for about R300 a month, or what they can afford [MS].
- Donations of any amount, which the Sophia Foundation matches [MS]
- Volunteers for the monthly alien hack [MS]

## Offers
- **The Greyton Nature Programme.** Reserve management, trail maintenance and signs, a team of rangers who help people and wildlife (including baboons) live side by side, environmental education, clearing of invasive plants, and biodiversity monitoring [MS]
- **The monthly alien hack.** Residents and visitors clear invasive plants from priority areas for two hours on the first Sunday of each month, followed by tea [MS]
- **Greyton Village Market.** Every Saturday from 09:00 to 12:00. Most stallholders live in the Overberg and make or grow what they sell, and the market helps fund the society [MS].
- **Trails.** Walking, running, hiking, and mountain-biking trails in the reserve and on the commonage [MS]
- **Village character.** Committees that work with the municipality to protect the look and feel of the village [MS]
- **A say for members.** Members attend the annual general meeting, set policy, and elect the committee [MS]
