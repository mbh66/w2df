## What belongs here

Work about making, growing, or selling things: a farm's harvest, a guesthouse, a craft business, a community garden.

If your project is mainly about rules or rights (a land dispute, a municipal service), it belongs under [[civic/index|Civic]]. If it is mainly about learning or culture (a training course, a museum), it belongs under [[cultural/index|Cultural]].
