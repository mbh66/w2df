The Greyton Village Market is held every Saturday morning from 09:00 to 12:00, all year round, at the market venue on Main Road, Greyton [MS]. The [[greyton-conservation-society|Greyton Conservation Society]] runs it [MS]. It began in the late 1970s as a single stall selling extra produce from local farms [MS]. Today residents and weekend visitors meet there for coffee, food, plants, and local products [MS].

Stallholders live in the Overberg and sell only what they grow, bake, brew, or make themselves. Resellers are not accepted [MS]. Each stallholder pays a small fee, which helps pay for the society's conservation programmes and the upkeep of the market [MS].

## Needs
- Local producers from the Overberg who want a stall [MS]
- Shoppers, since every stall fee supports conservation work around Greyton [MS]

## Offers
- **A weekly place to sell.** Stalls for Overberg growers, bakers, brewers, cooks, and makers [MS]
- **Local produce.** Bread, seedlings, plants and flowers, home cooking, and handmade goods [MS]
- **A weekly gathering.** Coffee and music between the stalls, for residents and visitors [MS]
- **Conservation society membership.** Supporter membership (R100) can be bought at the society's stand at the market [MS]

## How to get involved
To apply for a stall, contact the Greyton Conservation Society through its [market page](https://greytonconservationsociety.com/market/) or ask in the market's Facebook group. For anything else, contact the moderators via [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].
