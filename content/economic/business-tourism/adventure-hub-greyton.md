The Adventure Hub is a café, outdoor store, and information centre at 29 Main Road, Greyton [MS]. It helps visitors plan and book activities in Greyton and the wider Overberg, including mountain biking, hiking, trail running, and guided experiences [MS]. The ward has no working tourism office, and the Adventure Hub already serves as an information and support point for visitors [IC].

## Needs
- Local businesses that offer experiences in Greyton or the Overberg, to join as Hub Partners. The Adventure Hub markets and sells partners' experiences to its customers [MS].
- Listing on the Theewaterskloof Municipality website as a tourism information point. The Forum recorded this as an action at its first meeting, with the date still to be confirmed [IC].

## Offers
- **Nature experiences.** Bird walks and nature walks in the Greyton Nature Reserve led by a local expert, and half-day birding trips on farm roads beyond the village [MS]
- **Heritage walks.** A historic walk through Greyton, a walk through Genadendal's mission buildings and museum area, and a combined Genadendal and Greyton route with traditional food [MS]
- **Wine outings.** A picnic at a boutique farm near Greyton, and day trips to farms in Botrivier and the Hemel-en-Aarde Valley, with transport provided [MS]
- **Creative workshops.** Clay and ceramic-painting sessions for groups [MS]
- **Cycling and hiking.** Gravel-riding packages, including the two-day Greyton Grinder, and the Boesmanskloof hike [MS]
- **Scenic flights.** Private flights over the Overberg coast, the Cape Peninsula, and De Hoop Nature Reserve [MS]
- **The store.** Greyton-roasted coffee, snacks and grab-and-go meals, local retail brands, and mountain-biking and hiking gear [MS]
- **Local knowledge.** Information and tips for visitors on what to do in and around Greyton [MS]

## How to get involved
Visit the store on Main Road, book an experience, or apply to become a Hub Partner through the [Adventure Hub website](https://theadventurehub.co.za/become-a-hub-member/). For anything else, contact the moderators via [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].
