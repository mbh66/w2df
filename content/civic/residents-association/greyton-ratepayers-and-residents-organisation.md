The Greyton Ratepayers & Residents Organisation (GRRO) is a community organisation for ratepayers and residents [MS]. It serves the communities of Greyton and Heuwelkroon [MS]. It shares news and calls for community action through its Facebook page, and runs an email newsletter [MS].
