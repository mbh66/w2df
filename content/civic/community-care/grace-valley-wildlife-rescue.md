Grace Valley Wildlife Rescue is the wildlife division of [[greyton-farm-animal-sanctuary|Greyton Farm Animal Sanctuary]], and works under its non-profit registration (NPO 187-044, PBO 930070994) [MS]. Its training arm is called the Wild Ways Learning Centre [MS]. It started in 2020 [MS].

It responds to calls about injured, orphaned, and displaced wild animals, and stabilises them before they go to a permitted rehabilitation centre or a vet [MS]. It has responded to hundreds of wildlife emergencies across the Overberg, from bats and songbirds to raptors, mammals, reptiles, tortoises, and duikers [MS]. Its public Facebook group, started in 2021, has more than 770 members [MS].

In 2025 it announced plans to become a permitted satellite rehabilitation centre linked to the Kogelberg Biosphere Wildlife Rescue and Training Centre [MS].

## Needs
- Money to fit out its rehabilitation building with enclosures, quarantine areas, and safety systems. Its online fundraising campaign aims to raise R78,000 [MS].
- Building materials, such as roof sheets, window frames, insulation, and a double sink [MS]

## Offers
- **Rescue.** A first response to wild animals in trouble across the Overberg [MS]
- **Wildlife First Responder training.** Online courses that teach people how to safely check, stabilise, and hand over injured wild animals, with instruction sheets and a network of trainees. More than 200 people across South Africa have been trained [MS].
- **Community group.** A public Facebook group where residents can report animals in need and follow rescues [MS]
