The Greyton Animal Welfare Society (GAWS) is a registered non-profit run by volunteers (NPO 060-902) [MS]. Its office is at 33 Main Road, Greyton, and it serves Greyton, Genadendal, Voorstekraal, Bereaville, Heuwelkroon, Boschmanskloof, and the farms around them [MS].

## Needs
None stated yet.

## Offers
- Sterilisation of pets [MS]
- Rescue, fostering, and rehoming of animals [MS]
- A mobile clinic and animal healthcare [MS]
- Cruelty prevention and education [MS]
- Boarding [MS]
- A 24-hour emergency service [MS]
