The [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] in partnership with TWK LED coordinated a small business entrepreneurship training course at the Hester Dorothea Conference Centre in Genadendal [MS]. It supplied light meals, tea and coffee for participants [MS]. The [[organisations/small-enterprise-development-and-finance-agency|Small Enterprise Development and Finance Agency]] (SEDFA) awarded the training contract to Eagle Marketing, a training service provider [MS]. The DevForum helped with the planning behind the scenes [MS]. About 25 people from across the valley took part [MS].

The course ran over two weeks [MS]:

| Dates | Modules |
| --- | --- |
| 13 to 15 October 2026 | 1. Identify and demonstrate entrepreneurial ideas and opportunities<br>2. Determine the financial requirements of a new venture<br>3. Match a new venture opportunity to market needs |
| 20 and 21 October 2026 | 4. Apply the principles of costing and pricing<br>5. Manage finances for a new venture |

## What came of it

About twenty people completed the course and received certificates [MS].

## What comes next

Participants who wanted to explore a community-wide co-operative met again on 5 November 2026: [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-05-co-operative-follow-up-meeting|Co-operative Follow-up Meeting, Voorstekraal]].

On 12 November 2026, participants attended a supplier open day and CV workshop in Caledon: [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-12-msme-supplier-open-day-caledon|MSME Supplier Open Day, Caledon]].
