Members of the [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-10-27-places-conversation|Places Conversation]] visited Genadendal to see the valley as a possible prototype for place-based investment and academic partnership [MS]. The morning ran from 10:00 to 13:00. An optional lunch followed, and the day ended by 14:00 [MS].

## What came of it

Ward 2 Development Forum presented a program plan for the valley.
Ranyaka presented their SWARM methodology and invited participants to register on their community engagement platform.
Stellenbosch University agreed to plan an academic program for the valley.
