[[case-studies/bonnievale-418|Bonnievale 418]], "in partnership with Professor Bernie Van Zijl", is holding a one-day Skills & Enterprise Development Workshop at Character Hall, Jakes Gerwel Technical, in Bonnievale [MS]. It starts at 09:00 for 09:30 [MS]. The keynote speaker is Jaco Londt, the Western Cape Minister of Education [IC]. The invitation says the workshop will bring communities, funders and stakeholders together to show work already happening in different communities, meet funders and build partnerships [MS]. The DevForum's convenor was invited and will attend to represent the DevForum [MS]. RSVPs close on 15 November 2026 [MS].

Bonnievale 418 grew out of the town's no-fee technical high school, which the [[concepts/valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] draws on. More on how it works: [[case-studies/bonnievale-418|Bonnievale 418]].
