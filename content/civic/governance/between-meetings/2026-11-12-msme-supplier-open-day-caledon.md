People who completed the [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-10-13-small-business-entrepreneurship-training|Small Business Entrepreneurship Training]] attended an MSME Supplier Open Day at Victoria Hall in Caledon [MS]. [[organisations/theewaterskloof-municipality|Theewaterskloof Municipality]] and Klipheuwel Wind Farm, part of [[organisations/globeleq|Globeleq]], hosted the open days in partnership with Elgin Community College [MS]. The municipality's Local Economic Development office arranged for participants from the valley to attend, and the college sent a bus to collect them early in the morning [MS]. 

## What the open days offer

The invitation describes the open days as a place for small businesses to meet public and private support bodies, learn how to become a supplier or service provider to either sector, and find out how to get financial and non-financial support [MS]. The support bodies listed include several banks, the [[organisations/small-enterprise-development-and-finance-agency|Small Enterprise Development and Finance Agency]] (SEDFA), the Department of Employment and Labour, the South African Revenue Service (SARS) and the National Home Builders Registration Council (NHBRC) [MS]. They were open to local businesses, entrepreneurs, small and medium enterprises, service providers and suppliers [MS]. The municipality and Klipheuwel held similar open days in Caledon and Grabouw in November 2025 [MS].
