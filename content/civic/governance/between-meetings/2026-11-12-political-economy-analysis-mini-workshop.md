The DevForum's convenor met online with a Greyton resident who spent about three decades running conservation and climate programmes funded by international donors [MS]. The resident had shared three papers on the method, and answered questions as the convenor worked through how to apply it in Ward 2 and how it fits the principles of the [BioConomy wiki](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/) [MS]. A political economy analysis looks behind a problem to find out who wants what, and why.

## What came of it

The convenor decided [MS]:

- that the analysis will start from the DevForum's [[priorities/index|five priorities]], and any programme will follow from what it finds;
- that it will be the second research run promised on [[challenges|Challenges]], using the carrying budget already set out there;
- that its question will go to the Genadendal Transformasie Komitee in writing first, and then to a DevForum meeting.

Draft pages followed on the same day: [[political-economy-of-the-valley|Political Economy of the Valley]], and research pages for the three papers in the [[research/index|Research Register]] [MS]. The method is explained on the BioConomy wiki at [Political Economy Analysis](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/political-economy-analysis).

## What comes next

The moderators will agree the terms of the work and send them to the Komitee, with an Afrikaans copy. Conversations across the valley are planned for January and February 2027, after the holidays.
