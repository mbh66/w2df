People who completed the [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-10-13-small-business-entrepreneurship-training|Small Business Entrepreneurship Training]] met at the Voorstekraal Community Hall to ask whether they want to plan a co-operative together [MS]. The chair of the [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] attended [MS], and the DevForum's convenor explained what a shared co-operative brand could do for the group [MS]. The meeting cost nothing: community members gave their time as mentors, and the DevForum provided coffee and biscuits [MS].

The group now keeps its own record on the Koop site, including who took part and the full notes: [Meeting of 5 November 2026](https://koop.valleyofgrace.co.za/meetings/2026-11-05-meeting).

## What came of it

The group decided [MS]:

- to meet once a week;
- to hold its next meeting on Sunday 8 November 2026, with each person bringing their own idea or business plan on paper;
- that two people from each community will bring formal proposals for the future co-operative's board.

The group also asked for Theewaterskloof Municipality to attend a later meeting and to bring [[organisations/small-enterprise-development-and-finance-agency|SEDFA]] and the Department of Agriculture to explain how to start a co-operative [MS].

## What comes next

The group will show its garden in Voorstekraal on 18 November 2026: [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-18-garden-visit-voorstekraal|Garden Visit, Voorstekraal]]. Its weekly meetings are recorded on the Koop site: [Weekly Meetings](https://koop.valleyofgrace.co.za/meetings/). The idea itself, as it will be tabled at the DevForum, is on [[concepts/valley-of-grace-agri-heritage-co-operative|Valley of Grace Agri-Heritage Co-operative]].
