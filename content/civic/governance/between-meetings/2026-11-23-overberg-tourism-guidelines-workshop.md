The [[organisations/western-cape-department-of-economic-development-and-tourism|Western Cape Department of Economic Development and Tourism]] has drafted Guidelines for Managing Regional and Local Tourism in the Western Cape, and is holding a workshop in each district to test them [MS]. The Overberg workshop is hosted with the [[organisations/overberg-district-municipality|Overberg District Municipality]] at the Overstrand Municipal Auditorium in Hermanus, from 09:30 to 15:00 [MS]. HS Business Solutions facilitates it on the department's behalf [MS]. The DevForum's convenor was invited and will attend to represent the DevForum [MS].

## What the workshop covers

- The roles of municipalities, the Regional Tourism Organisation, Local Tourism Offices and Local Tourism Associations [MS].
- How tourism bodies are governed and funded, including National Treasury's [[rules/mfma-circular-131|MFMA Circular 131]] and what it means for municipal tourism funding [MS].
- The models the draft Guidelines propose for managing tourism. In group work, participants assess each model and say which they prefer and why [MS].

The department says the input will be used to finalise the Guidelines and plan how they are put into practice [MS]. Municipal officials and tourism bodies are invited, and it expects 30 to 50 people [MS].

## Why it matters to the valley

[[priorities/tourism-development|Tourism Development]] is one of the DevForum's five priorities. At its [[civic/governance/minutes/2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|inaugural meeting]], the DevForum noted that the ward has no working tourism office [MS]. The Guidelines will shape how a local tourism office or association for the valley could be set up and funded.
