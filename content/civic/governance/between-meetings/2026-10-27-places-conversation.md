Johan Olivier of [[organisations/ranyaka-community-transformation|Ranyaka]] brought nine people together to discuss his concept note, "A Third Way of Investing in Places" [MS]. The note names Genadendal and Greyton as one of three places under consideration for a demonstration [MS].

## What came of it

Everyone present said they want to work toward place-based economies and agreed to visit the valley as a possible prototype [MS]. Who took part, and what each might offer and need: [[organisations/ranyaka-places-conversation|Ranyaka Places Conversation: Offers and Needs]].
