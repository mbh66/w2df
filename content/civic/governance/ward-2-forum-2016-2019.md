The Ward 2 Forum was a community forum for Ward 2 of Theewaterskloof Municipality, which ran from about 2016 to 2019 [MS]. It published the website ward2forum.org, now available only through the Internet Archive [IC]. Between March and May 2017 it published *Farm 39 Transformation: The Lost 18 Years*, a series on the stalled transfer of [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] under [[rules/trancraa|TRANCRAA]] [IC]. The series argued that "the requirements of the community, and not those of TWKM, should be paramount" [MS], and that the [[civic/governance/genadendal-transformasie-komitee|Transformasie Komitee]] had properly represented the community [MS]. The Forum also kept a page of legal reference documents on the transfer, including the Komitee's constitution and the 2008 settlement made an order of the High Court [IC]. The present [[civic/governance/ward-2-development-forum|Ward 2 Development Forum]] relies on that archive for its own pages on Farm 39 [MS].

The Forum is no longer active [MS]. Who led it, what else it worked on, and why it stopped are not yet recorded on this site.
