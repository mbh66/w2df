The Ward 2 Business and Community Development Forum (W2DF) is a ward-level forum for Genadendal, Greyton, Heuwelkroon, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, Boschmanskloof, and the surrounding farms [MS]. It brings together local businesses, community organisations, commercial and emerging farmers, tourism operators, conservation groups, schools and training bodies, and government departments [MS]. It held its first meeting on 16 September 2026 at the Hester Dorothea Conference Centre in Genadendal [MS].

The Forum is run by the community, and its members set its priorities [MS]. Theewaterskloof Municipality gives administrative support at first, with minutes, contact lists, and venues [MS]. The Forum treats the valley as one area and works for both Greyton and Genadendal together [MS]. The Transformasie Komitee takes part as a stakeholder [MS].

The Forum agreed that affected communities should be involved in proposals from the start, and not only once a proposal has been written [MS]. It will not duplicate other bodies that already deal with the TRANCRAA land process. It will take practical planning matters, such as land use, zoning, and building plans, to the municipality's operational officials [MS].

This website is the Forum's public register of initiatives in the ward.

## Needs
- A champion for each of the five priorities, to be named at the November 2026 meeting [MS]
- People from every settlement and sector to take part in meetings [MS]
- The Transformasie Komitee's 8-sector plan, to be considered alongside the Forum's priorities [MS]
- Entries for this register: initiatives, businesses, groups, and services in the ward. See [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].

## Offers
- **Five development priorities** for the ward: tourism development, education and skills, agriculture and food sovereignty, conservation and environment, and business development and local procurement. See [[priorities/index|Forum priorities]] [MS].
- **Meetings every two months**, with minutes published on this site [MS]
- **A route to government.** The Forum invites municipal and provincial officials to its meetings, and can take ward matters to the provincial Overberg Business Networking Forum [MS].
- **A skills-needs list** for the ward, being compiled from farmers and tourism operators [MS]
- **This register**, where initiatives in the ward can list what they need and what they offer

## How to get involved
Come to the next Forum meeting in November 2026. The date and venue will be announced on the [[civic/governance/minutes/index|Forum Minutes]] page. To ask a question or add an initiative, see the [[contact|Contact]] page.
