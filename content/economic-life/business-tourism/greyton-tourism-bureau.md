The Greyton Tourism Bureau runs a visitor information office at 29 Main Road, Greyton, and the website greytontourism.com [MS]. It helps visitors find things to do, places to stay and eat, local businesses, and upcoming events [MS]. The office is open Tuesday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00. It is closed on Sunday and Monday [MS].

## Needs
- Local businesses to list in its online directory. A basic listing is free. A premium listing, with its own page, photos, and links, costs R240 a month [MS].
- Organisers of local events to list their events on the website [MS]

## Offers
- **Visitor information.** Help with things to do, accommodation, restaurants, hiking trails, markets, and planning weddings [MS]
- **Business directory.** Listings for accommodation, food and drink, shops, art and workshops, and health and beauty businesses in Greyton [MS]
- **Events calendar.** Festivals and events in and around Greyton, with tickets for some events sold on the website [MS]. It lists the Greyton Saturday market (09:30 to 12:00) and the Genadendal night market (the first Friday of each month) [MS].
- **Outdoor information.** Hiking and mountain-biking routes, a downloadable hiking map, information on the Boesmanskloof trail and CapeNature permits, and links to local alien-clearing groups [MS]
- **Village history and map.** A history of Greyton from the Hassequa Khoikhoi to the founding of the village in 1854, and a downloadable village map [MS]

## How to get involved
List your business or event through the [Greyton Tourism website](https://greytontourism.com/membership-account/membership-levels/), or visit the office on Main Road. For anything else, contact the moderators via [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].
