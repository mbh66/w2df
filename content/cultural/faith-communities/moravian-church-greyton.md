Greyton has a small Moravian mission church [IC]. The building was first a Dutch Reformed church [IC]. Theewaterskloof Municipality's planning documents list the Moravian church and the original Moravian school among Greyton's historic features [IC]. The old Moravian school building is now the town library [IC].
