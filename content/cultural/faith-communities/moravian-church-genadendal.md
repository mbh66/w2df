The Moravian Church in Genadendal is the congregation of the first Moravian mission in South Africa, which Georg Schmidt started in 1738 [IC]. It belongs to the Overberg district of the Moravian Church in South Africa [MS]. Its church stands on Church Square (die Werf), and is still used for worship [MS]. The church houses the oldest pipe organ in South Africa [MS].

The mission buildings, including the church square and its bell tower, form the Genadendal Mission Complex, a Provincial Heritage Site [IC]. In 2038 Genadendal will mark 300 years since the mission was founded [IC].
