Bereaville has a Moravian mission church, as one of the villages linked to the Genadendal mission [MS]. Moravian missionaries started a school in Bereaville in 1865, which is now [[berea-primary|Berea Primary School]] [MS]. The congregation's current details still need to be confirmed [TBV].
