Greyton MTB Juniors is a youth programme linked to the Greyton Mountain Bike Club, and has been running for about ten years [MS]. It takes about 75 boys and girls on weekly rides on the trails around Greyton [MS]. Girls ride on Tuesdays, and boys on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons. Each ride lasts about 90 minutes and ends with juice and a sandwich, before the bikes are washed and packed away [MS]. Every child wears a helmet on every ride, and each has been given a cycling top, paid for by donations from other cyclists [MS].

Most of the programme's money comes from its yearly Canola Roller event [MS].

## Needs
- Helmets, so that each child can have their own [MS]
- Enough bikes for all the children to ride at the same time [MS]
- Money for repairs, since most of the bikes are old and often fixed [MS]
- Adult riders to join the Tuesday or Thursday rides, including visitors [MS]

## Offers
- Weekly rides, skills training, and mentorship for young people [MS]
- A cycling top for each child [MS]
