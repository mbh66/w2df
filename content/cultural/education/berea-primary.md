Berea (Mor) Primary School is a public primary school in Kerkstraat, Bereaville [IC]. It teaches Grade R to Grade 7 and serves Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and the farms around them [MS]. It had 274 learners in 2026 [IC]. It is a quintile 1 school, so learners pay no school fees [IC].

Moravian missionaries started the school in 1865. The first 12 learners were taught in a room of a church house [MS].
