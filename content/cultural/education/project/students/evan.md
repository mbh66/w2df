## Your question

How does a bond turn clean water into something investors will pay for? Who pays whom?

Your hands-first job is to invent the Bean Bond game, so you can play the bank.

## Facts to meet

Choose three of these for your Field Book. Tie a thread to each one.

- A bond is a loan. A bank or a government borrows money from many investors and promises to pay it back with extra.
- The Cape Water Performance-Based Bond was listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange on 17 April 2026. It is worth R2.5 billion and was issued by [[organisations/firstrand-bank|FirstRand Bank]]. [IC]
- Part of what the bond pays investors depends on checked results from clearing invasive plants in Cape Town's mountain catchments. [IC]
- [[organisations/rand-merchant-bank|Rand Merchant Bank]] put the bond together. Three big investors joined first: the [[organisations/international-finance-corporation|International Finance Corporation]], [[organisations/fsd-africa-investments|FSD Africa Investments]] and [[organisations/aluwani-capital-partners|Aluwani Capital Partners]]. [MS]
- [[organisations/the-nature-conservancy-south-africa|The Nature Conservancy South Africa]] does the restoration work. It passes about USD 8.8 million over five years to the [[organisations/greater-cape-town-water-fund|Greater Cape Town Water Fund]]. [MS]
- The Water Fund says clearing 54,300 hectares in seven mountain catchments would add 55 billion litres of water a year within six years, at about a tenth of what desalination costs for each litre. [MS]
- Nobody has asked the bond's organisers whether work in our valley could count. [TBV]

**Words to meet:** investor (someone who lends money hoping to earn more back), issuer (the bank that creates the bond and sells it), performance-based (the payment depends on a result that is measured), desalination (taking salt out of seawater).

## Think of Europe

Traders on Europe's rivers paid fees at certain places. Who got paid, and for what? Now think about our valley. When water flows from our mountains to the sea, who gets paid? Who should? Bring your answer from main lesson.

## Week 1

You have about two hours on your own.

**Do**

1. **Invent the Bean Bond (40 minutes).** With a parent, design the game on one sheet of paper. You need four players: the Bank (you), the Investors (your family), a Restoration Team and a Checker. The investors hand beans to the bank. The bank hands some beans to the restoration team to do a job. The bank promises the investors their beans back plus a bonus, but only if the checker confirms the job was done. Decide what the job is, how many beans each person gets, and what the bonus is. All numbers are pretend.
2. **Play one round at home (30 minutes).** Use dried beans. The job could be pulling the weeds from one flowerbed. A parent plays the checker. Notice what happens when the checker says the job is not done.

**Tell**

3. **Tell it back (10 minutes).** Tell a grown-up how the beans moved. Use the beans to show them.

**Think**

4. **Your Field Book page (20 minutes).** Draw the boxes: Investors, the Bond, the Water Fund, the clearing teams and the City of Cape Town. Draw an arrow for each payment you know about and label it. Use a solid line for an arrow you have a fact for. Use a dotted line for an arrow you are guessing. Count your dotted lines. Those are your questions.
5. **Three facts (10 minutes).** Choose three facts from the list and tie a thread to each: blue, yellow or red.
6. **Think of Europe (10 minutes).** Write two sentences in answer to the question above.
