## Your question

Who would do the restoration work, and what would they need to learn?

You are the class expert on the people who clear, plant and watch over the land.

## What you need to know

Water does not come back to a stream by itself. Someone has to cut down the thirsty invasive trees and plant native plants in their place. Someone has to keep watch over the land to see whether it is working. That is the work, and it needs people.

### Two valley groups that do this work

**Wild Restoration (Greyton).** Wild Restoration has a clearing team drawn from the local community. It also runs volunteer days. It supports private landowners, and it monitors the land with trail cameras and recordings. You will stamp these facts **Their own word**, because they come from the group's own Register page.

**Zonderend Water Users Association.** The Association clears river banks. It also runs a nursery in Genadendal, where a team of 14 people grows palmiet plants. The team is employed through the Expanded Public Works Programme, a government scheme that pays people for work that helps the community. You will stamp these facts **Their own word**.

### What nobody knows yet

The Forum has an idea for a water skills course at the Valley of Grace Learning Campus. That idea has not been agreed yet. You will stamp it **Not yet checked**. Nobody has decided who would teach it or who would take it.

### Words to know

- **Restoration:** helping damaged land recover.
- **Nursery:** a place where young plants are grown.
- **Monitoring:** watching and recording what changes over time.
- **Volunteer:** someone who helps without being paid.

## The three stamps

- **Checked twice:** two separate sources agree, or an official record says so.
- **Their own word:** it comes from the person or group the fact is about.
- **Not yet checked:** it seems likely, but nobody has confirmed it.

## Week 1 homework

This should take about two hours.

**1. Read this page twice (15 minutes).** Underline every word you do not know.

**2. You are guessing, so stamp every picture **Not yet checked**. In Week 3 you may visit or interview someone, and you can fix your drawings then.
