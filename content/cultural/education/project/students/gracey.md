This is your own page for the Water for the Valley project. New work is added above the old work each week. Right now we're at Week 1.

You may use an AI helper. Copy and paste this link into the chat: https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/cultural/education/project/llms.txt. It can check a fact or explain a hard word. Your Field Book is yours to draw and write.

## Your question

Who would do the restoration work, and what would they need to learn?

You are the class expert on the people who clear, plant and watch over the land. Your hands-first job is to try a small piece of the work yourself.

## Facts to meet

Choose three of these for your Field Book. Tie a thread to each one.

- [[wild-restoration|Wild Restoration]] has a clearing team drawn from the local community. It also runs volunteer clearing days. [MS]
- Wild Restoration watches the land with trail cameras and sound recordings. [MS]
- The [[zonderend-water-users-association|Zonderend Water Users Association]] clears river banks. It runs a nursery in Genadendal that grows palmiet and other local plants. [MS]
- A team of 14 people, employed through the Expanded Public Works Programme, runs the nursery. [MS]
- The Forum has an idea for a water skills course at the Valley of Grace Learning Campus. It has not been agreed. [TBV]

**Words to meet:** restoration (helping damaged land recover), nursery (a place where young plants are grown), monitoring (watching and recording what changes over time), volunteer (someone who helps without being paid).

## Think of Europe

Someone had to keep Europe's river banks, canals and roads in good order. Who did the keeping, and who paid for it? Now think about our valley. Who keeps our rivers and mountains in good order? Bring your answer from main lesson.

## Week 1

You have about two hours on your own.

**Do**

1. **Try the work (50 minutes).** With a parent, spend about 30 minutes pulling weeds by hand in a garden or on a walk. Ask the grown-up to show you which plants are weeds. Then plant one small seedling or cutting in a pot. Use your hands and a trowel only. No saws, knives or sprays. Keep a Work Diary as you go: what was hard, what hurt, what you needed to know.
2. **Draw a working day (25 minutes).** Draw four pictures of a day in the life of a worker on a clearing team, from early morning to the end of the day. You are guessing, so use a red thread. You will check your drawings with a real worker in Week 3.

**Tell**

3. **Tell it back (15 minutes).** Tell a grown-up what the work felt like in your body. Say what you would need to learn to do it well.

**Think**

4. **Your Field Book page (10 minutes).** Stick in your best drawing and one line from your Work Diary.
5. **Three facts (10 minutes).** Choose three facts from the list and tie a thread to each: blue, yellow or red.
6. **Think of Europe (10 minutes).** Write two sentences in answer to the question above.

## Bring to Week 2

Your Work Diary, your drawing, your Field Book page and your answer about Europe.
