# Fact or Hope: ten statements about the valley's water

For Week 1 of Water for the Valley. Part 1 is for the children. Part 2 is the guide's key.

## Part 1: Statements for the children

How to play: read each statement aloud. Each child holds up the stamp card they would give it: **Checked twice**, **Their own word**, or **Not yet checked**. Ask one child to say what would move it up a level. Then reveal the key.

1. Theewaterskloof Dam holds more water than any other dam that supplies Cape Town.
2. The Cape Water Performance-Based Bond was listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange in April 2026.
3. Clearing invasive trees from mountain streams puts water back into the streams.
4. Wild Restoration says more than 150 volunteers have joined its clearing days.
5. The Zonderend Water Users Association's nursery in Genadendal employs a team of 14.
6. The Moravian Church bought the mountain farm Langgezocht in 1899 to protect Genadendal's water.
7. Clearing invasive plants from 54,300 hectares in seven mountain catchments would add 55 billion litres of water a year within six years.
8. Restoring the valley's catchments would add water to the Riviersonderend.
9. The people who run Theewaterskloof Dam would agree to count the valley's water towards Cape Town's supply.
10. The organisers of the bond would be interested in work in our valley.

## Things to draw out in the game

- **Statement 7 sounds scientific, and its source is the group that wants the clearing paid for.** That makes it [MS], and the children can ask who else has measured it.
- **Statement 3 is [IC] and statement 8 is [TBV], though they sound alike.** Clearing helps streams in general. Nobody has measured what it would add in this valley. Ask the children where the difference lies.
- **Statements 9 and 10 are the hopes the whole project rests on.** Point them out. Part 8 of the final story names them as open questions.
- **Statements 4, 5 and 6 are about the valley's own organisations.** An organisation's own word is a good place to start. Ask what a second source would look like.
