## Your question

How does a bond turn clean water into something investors will pay for? Who pays whom?

## What you need to know

A bond is a loan. A bank or a government borrows money from many investors and promises to pay it back with extra. The extra is the investors' reward.

The Cape Water Performance-Based Bond was listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange on 17 April 2026. It is worth R2.5 billion and was issued by FirstRand Bank. Part of what it pays investors depends on checked results from clearing invasive plants in Cape Town's mountain catchments. You will stamp these facts **Checked twice**.

### Who is in it

The BioConomy wiki names these players. You will stamp each one **Their own word**, because we have only one source. Please check them again before we present.

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| Rand Merchant Bank | Put the bond together |
| International Finance Corporation, FSD Africa Investments and Aluwani Capital Partners | Big investors who joined first |
| The Nature Conservancy South Africa | Does the restoration work. Passes about USD 8.8 million over five years to the Greater Cape Town Water Fund |
| Conservation Alpha | Checks the results |
| Greater Cape Town Water Fund | Started in 2018 by the Nature Conservancy and the City of Cape Town |

### The Water Fund's big claim

The Fund says that clearing invasive plants from 54,300 hectares in seven mountain catchments would add 55 billion litres of water a year within six years. It says this would cost roughly a tenth of what desalination costs for each litre. Desalination means taking salt out of seawater. You will stamp this **Their own word**, because it is the Fund's own business case.

### What nobody knows yet

The bond pays for work in Cape Town's catchments. Nobody has asked the bond's organisers whether work in our valley could count. You will stamp this **Not yet checked**.

### Words to know

- **Investor:** a person or company that lends money hoping to earn more back.
- **Issuer:** the bank that creates the bond and sells it.
- **Performance-based:** the payment depends on a result that is measured.
- **Hectare:** an area the size of about one rugby field.

## Who you will study

All outside the Register: FirstRand Bank, Rand Merchant Bank, the three big investors, the Nature Conservancy South Africa, Conservation Alpha, the Greater Cape Town Water Fund and the City of Cape Town.

## The three stamps

- **Checked twice:** two separate sources agree, or an official record says so.
- **Their own word:** it comes from the person or group the fact is about.
- **Not yet checked:** it seems likely, but nobody has confirmed it.
