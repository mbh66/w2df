Farm 38, Boschmans Kloof, is one of the mountain farms above Greyton in the Caledon registration division [IC]. It lies in the mountains north-east of [[villages/greyton|Greyton]], in the upper kloof of the Gobos River [MS]. The Forum's land table lists Farms 32 to 38 together as [[organisations/capenature|CapeNature]] land [MS]. Little else about its history has been recorded yet, and this page sets out what is known and what is still open.

**Map:** see this property on the Forum's [Google Earth map of the valley's land](https://earth.google.com/earth/d/1PejJJkRHA_WrwYwdKrSeea92u3q3vp9y?usp=sharing), which shows the Surveyor-General boundaries of all eight properties.

## The name

The farm shares its name with the kloof, and with the settlement of [[villages/boschmanskloof|Boschmanskloof]] on the edge of Greyton. They are different places: the settlement is part of Genadendal in the municipality's spatial plan [IC] and stands on Farm 39 land [MS], while Farm 38 is mountain land.

## History

- **Before Greyton.** Greyton sources say the Hessequa leader [[history/people/stoffel-koekson|Stoffel Koekson]] moved his people to Boschmanskloof in the late 1600s [TBV], and that [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] first settled on a farm at Boschmanskloof before he bought Weltevreden in 1846 [TBV]. Whether either account refers to Farm 38, or to land lower down where the settlement stands, has not been established.
- **The trail.** CapeNature publishes the Boesmanskloof hiking trail between McGregor and Greyton [IC]. The trail crosses Farm 38, and Breakfast Rock is in Farm 38.
- **The mountain commonage.** Greyton's histories say [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] left the land around the village to its plot owners as a commonage [TBV]. On 13 May 1977 the Greyton Nature Reserve, about 2,200 hectares, was proclaimed on the "mountain commonage", up to the peaks of Uitkyk, Wa en Osse and Perdekop [IC].
- **Who manages the mountain.** The register's sources do not agree. One records that the [[economic/farmers-land-water/greyton-conservation-society|Greyton Conservation Society]] helps Theewaterskloof Municipality manage the reserve [MS]. The Forum's land table lists Farms 32 to 38 as CapeNature land, with the reserve covering about 2,200 hectares of them [MS]. Whether Farm 38 is part of the reserve, the commonage, or other CapeNature land has not been confirmed [TBV].
- **The 2019 fire.** A fire burned about 5,950 hectares on the mountain slopes above Greyton in December 2019 [IC]. Whether it reached Farm 38 has not been checked.
- **Extension.** The Greyton Conservation Society says CapeNature's stewardship panel has approved a proposed extension of the reserve [MS].

## On the Forum's map

- The farm lies in the mountains north-east of [[villages/greyton|Greyton]], in the upper kloof of the Gobos River [MS].
- Two of the three dam-wall options drawn on the Gobos in the Forum's map (G1 and G2) fall inside Farm 38 [MS]. These are options for the idea on [[concepts/community-built-dams-on-the-baviaans-and-gobos|Community-Built Dams on the Baviaans and Gobos]], which the Forum has not agreed.
- The upper Gobos feeds Greyton's leiwater, which has carried water to the village's plots since 1854 [MS]. See [[history/leiwater|Greyton's Leiwater]].

## What is still open

- Who holds title to Farm 38, and since when.
- Whether it was ever part of Greyton's commonage, or of the farm Weltevreden.
- Whether it lies inside the Greyton Nature Reserve or the Riviersonderend Complex.
- What the date on the Surveyor-General record (2018-05-29) marks.
