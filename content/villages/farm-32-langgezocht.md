Langgezocht is Farm 32 in the Caledon registration division: the mountain land behind [[villages/genadendal|Genadendal]], where the Baviaans River rises [MS]. The Moravian Church bought it in 1899 to protect the water sources of [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] [MS]. The Forum's land table records it as [[organisations/capenature|CapeNature]] land today [MS]. How and when it changed hands has not yet been confirmed.

**Map:** see this property on the Forum's [Google Earth map of the valley's land](https://earth.google.com/earth/d/1PejJJkRHA_WrwYwdKrSeea92u3q3vp9y?usp=sharing), which shows the Surveyor-General boundaries of all eight properties.

## History

- **1899.** The Moravian Church bought Langgezocht "in order to protect the water sources of Farm 39" [MS]. The Ward 2 Forum's 2017 series and the register's history pages both give it as 1,881 hectares [MS].
- **Who bought it.** The mission land below it was held by the church in trust for residents from 1858 [MS]. Langgezocht was a separate purchase, so it was never part of that trust land [MS]. *Inference:* it therefore sits outside the TRANCRAA process for Farm 39.
- **After 1899.** No record has yet been found of when the farm left the church, or how it came to CapeNature [TBV].
- **Today.** The Forum's land table lists Farms 32 to 38 together as CapeNature land, 5,230.29 hectares [MS]. Any work on Langgezocht needs CapeNature's approval [MS].

## On the Forum's map

- The farm lies north of Farm 39, in the mountains above Genadendal [MS].
- Three of the four dam-wall options drawn on the Baviaans in the Forum's map (B2, B3 and B4) fall inside Langgezocht [MS]. These are options drawn for the idea on [[concepts/community-built-dams-on-the-baviaans-and-gobos|Community-Built Dams on the Baviaans and Gobos]], which the Forum has not agreed.
