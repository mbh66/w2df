Boschmanskloof is a small, close-knit community near Greyton. Its history reaches back before Greyton was founded. In the late 1600s the Hassequa Khoikhoi leader [[history/people/stoffel-koekson|Stoffel Koekson]] moved his people to Boschmanskloof and built mud-brick houses there. Their foundations are said to lie under the old houses of Boschmanskloof today.
