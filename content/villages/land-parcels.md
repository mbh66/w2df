Five parcels of land matter to many of the questions the DevForum is working on. They are Farm 32, Farm 39, Farm 827, Erf 77 and Erf 595. This page gives each one's Surveyor-General (SG) code and size, says what the register already records about it, and lists what nobody has yet confirmed. It records land. It makes no proposal about any parcel.

## Two sizes that do not match

Two figures differ from sizes the register has used before.

- **Erf 595.** The supplied area converts to 404.89 ha. The register's land table gives 1,404.89 ha, and the siting study's polygon is named with the same figure [MS]. The two differ by exactly 1,000 ha. One of them probably carries a typing error, but this page does not say which. Until a moderator checks the Surveyor-General record, treat the size as unconfirmed [TBV]. The Ohana environmental report of 2022 gives about 1,425 ha [MS].
- **Farm 39.** The supplied area is 4,495.95 ha. The siting study's boundary polygon is named at 4,498.09 ha [MS]. The difference is 2.14 ha, which could come from how each source measured the boundary.

## The parcels

### Farm 32

SG code C01300000000003200000, 1,835.09 ha [MS]. The register's research brief refers to it as Langgezocht, one of the farms that passed to CapeNature along with Farms 33 to 38 [MS]. When and how that happened is an open question on the *Challenges* page [MS].

### Farm 39

SG code C01300000000003900000, 4,495.95 ha [MS]. Farm 39 is the legal name for the land on which Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal and Boschmanskloof stand, and Madiba Park also stands on it [MS]. The Remainder of Farm 39 is held by the national Minister in trust, and the process under TRANCRAA for deciding who will own it has been open since the transitional period ended in 2011 [IC]. Anything touching this land goes to the Transformasie Komitee first.

Full account: [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] and [[civic/residents-association/trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA and Farm 39: The Land Transfer Process]].

### Farm 827

SG code C01300000000082700000, 61.10 ha [MS]. The register holds no page or record on this parcel. Its owner, zoning, use and relation to the other four parcels are not known to the register [TBV].

### Erf 77

SG code C01300080000007700000, 82.62 ha [MS]. The register holds no page or record on this parcel. Its owner, zoning and use are not known to the register. The fifth to eighth digits of its SG code (0008) differ from Erf 595's (0011), which suggests the two sit in different townships or town areas. This is a reading of the code and has not been checked [TBV].

### Erf 595

SG code C01300110000059500000, 404.89 ha as supplied, with the size conflict set out above [MS]. Erf 595 lies in Greyton. The Western Cape Department of Environmental Affairs and Development Planning authorised housing for 333 homes on part of it, in Heuwelkroon, on 10 July 2025 [IC]. Most of the erf lies outside that housing site [MS]. Who holds title, what it is zoned for and how it is used today are open questions on the *Challenges* page [MS]. The register's land table of the valley's 11,194.38 ha includes it.

Related pages: [[villages/heuwelkroon|Heuwelkroon]], [[concepts/why-alien-clearing|Why Alien Clearing?]] and [[challenges|Challenges]].

## What is still open

1. What is the correct area of Erf 595: 404.89 ha or 1,404.89 ha? The Surveyor-General's record settles it.
2. Which of the two figures for Farm 39 (4,495.95 ha or 4,498.09 ha) should the register use, and why do they differ?
3. Who owns and manages Farm 827 and Erf 77, and how does each relate to the valley?
4. When and how did Farm 32 pass to CapeNature, and under what terms?
5. Do any of these parcels share boundaries, and do they appear in the register's land table of the valley? The table should list all five.
