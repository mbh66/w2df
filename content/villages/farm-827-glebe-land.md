The Glebe Land is the land at the heart of [[villages/genadendal|Genadendal]] that was granted to the Moravian Church in 1927 [IC]. In the Surveyor-General's records it is Farm 827 in the Caledon registration division, named "GLEBE LAND", with an area of 61.11 hectares [IC]. It lies around the old mission core, and it is held apart from the trust land of [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] [IC]. For this reason it is not part of the land whose transfer is being decided under TRANCRAA [IC].

**Map:** see this property on the Forum's [Google Earth map of the valley's land](https://earth.google.com/earth/d/1PejJJkRHA_WrwYwdKrSeea92u3q3vp9y?usp=sharing), which shows the Surveyor-General boundaries of all eight properties.

## History

- **1737 to 1858.** The mission at Baviaanskloof, later Genadendal, was founded in 1737, and its church, school, workshops and houses grew around Church Square [IC]. In 1858 the whole mission land was granted to the church "in trust for" its residents [MS]. The grant confirmed the institution's "full possession of the church, schools, workshops, houses, gardens, plantations of trees, water leadings and other property hitherto occupied by them" [MS].
- **1909 and 1924.** The Mission Stations and Communal Reserves Act of 1909 placed mission land under a management board, and a secular board replaced the missionaries in 1924 [IC]. Balie's history says the 1909 Act gave the mission title to its church, school and houses, and reserved the rest for registered occupiers [MS].
- **1927.** Title to the core mission areas of Genadendal and Bereaville was granted free of charge to the Moravian Church [IC]. The Ward 2 Forum's 2017 series, drawing on a 2000 report for the church by the Surplus People Project and the Legal Resources Centre, describes this as "nine portions of land, known as the Glebe Lands" [MS].
- **1952.** The state's deed of grant for Farm 39 (G19/1952) gives the farm's extent as 4,772.97 hectares [IC]. The consultants' 2010 report states that this figure excludes "the Glebe Land of 70,7795ha" [MS].
- **Farm 827 grows.** The Deeds Office search report attached to the 2008 court order records Portions 3 to 7 of Farm 39, about 2.34 hectares together, as "Consol to Farm 827" [IC]. *Inference:* small pieces of Farm 39 were added to the Glebe Land at some date before 2007.
- **1980 to 1991.** The buildings on Church Square were declared national monuments in 1980, and the Mission Museum opened in the 1838 college building in 1987 [MS]. The mission buildings around Church Square are now a Provincial Heritage Site [IC].
- **Today.** The Ward 2 Forum's 2017 series describes the trust lands as Farm 39 "excluding the Glebe Lands", which it says belong to the Moravian Church [MS].

## On the Forum's map

- Farm 827 is a single parcel around the mission core of Genadendal [MS]. The Forum's map point for the historic centre of Genadendal falls inside it [MS].
- The parcel does not reach Bereaville [MS]. *Inference:* if the 1927 grant included land in Bereaville, that land is either recorded under another number or no longer held as Glebe Land.

## What is still open

- Whether Farm 827 holds all nine of the 1927 portions, and where the Bereaville portions are recorded.
- Why the areas differ: 61.11 hectares on the Surveyor-General's record, 68 hectares in the 2017 series, and 70.7795 hectares in the consultants' report.
- When Portions 3 to 7 of Farm 39 were added to Farm 827, and on whose application.
- The title deed under which the church holds Farm 827.
- What the date on the Surveyor-General record (8 December 2025) marks.
