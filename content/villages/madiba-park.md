Madiba Park is a settlement in Ward 2. It borders [[villages/heuwelkroon|Heuwelkroon]], which is part of Greyton [MS]. It stands on [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] land, the land on which Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Boschmanskloof also stand [MS]. The municipality does not provide basic services here, so land and service matters fall to the Transformasie Komitee [MS].

Its place between Greyton and Genadendal is contested. [[madiba-park-land-and-services|Madiba Park: Land Status and Basic Services]] sets out the situation and will carry the accounts of those involved.
