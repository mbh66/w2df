Heuwelkroon, Boschmanskloof, and Madiba Park lie next to each other on the edge of Greyton [MS]. People who are new to the valley often see them as one neighbourhood of Greyton. On paper they are three different cases. Heuwelkroon is part of Greyton [IC]. Boschmanskloof is part of Genadendal [IC]. Madiba Park stands on Farm 39 land held in trust by the national government, and the municipality has said it cannot provide full services there because the land is not the municipality's [MS]. This page explains what that means for anyone who wants to help.

## The lay of the land

The [Greyton Governance Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1NhqnmXfc1PCWgRa5i8beRdVrW-j6ZDM), developed by W2DF, shows how closely the three settlements fit together [MS]. The sizes and distances below are this page's own measurements from that map.

![[greyton-governance-map.png|820]]

- **Heuwelkroon** covers about 19 hectares on the south-western edge of Greyton [MS].
- **Boschmanskloof** is a long, narrow strip of about 32 hectares on Greyton's western side, running about 1.6 kilometres north up the kloof [MS]. Its eastern edge borders Heuwelkroon for more than 400 metres, and Greyton for about 250 metres [MS].
- **Madiba Park** covers about 4 hectares at the southern tip of Boschmanskloof [MS]. It shares about 170 metres of boundary with Heuwelkroon and about 160 metres with Boschmanskloof [MS]. Its nearest edge is about 300 metres from Greyton [MS].

The three settlements meet near a single corner [MS]. From the middle of Madiba Park to the middle of Heuwelkroon is about 300 metres [MS].

## One ward, two kinds of land

Theewaterskloof Municipality is the local government for both Greyton and Genadendal [IC]. Ward 2 covers all seven settlements, including these three [MS]. What sets Heuwelkroon, Boschmanskloof, and Madiba Park apart is the land they rest on.

[[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] is the legal name for a piece of land [IC]. It has no council of its own [MS]. The national Minister responsible for land reform holds most of it in trust for the people who live on it [IC]. A law of 1998, known as [[trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA]], says this land must pass to an owner the residents help choose [IC]. That transfer has not happened [IC]. In 2004 residents elected the [[civic/governance/genadendal-transformasie-komitee|Transformasie Komitee]] to take part in the process for them [IC]. The full story is on [[civic/residents-association/trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA and Farm 39: The Land Transfer Process]].

Greyton was laid out as a freehold town, where owners hold title deeds [IC]. The municipality plans and builds on Greyton's land in the usual way. On Farm 39 it works with the Komitee and with the national department, which holds the land [IC].

## Heuwelkroon: part of Greyton

Heuwelkroon was built as a township on the edge of Greyton for coloured families who were forced out of Greyton under the Group Areas Act [IC]. Sources date the removals to between the late 1960s and the early 1970s [TBV]. Until the mid-1990s Heuwelkroon had its own management committee under Greyton's white town council [IC].

Today the municipality's spatial plan treats Heuwelkroon as part of Greyton, with its own business area and sports fields [IC]. The municipality can plan and build here. In July 2025 the province authorised it to build 333 homes on Erf 595 in Heuwelkroon [IC]. The story of the removals is on [[history/history-of-greyton|History of Greyton]].

## Boschmanskloof: next to Heuwelkroon, part of Genadendal

Boschmanskloof borders Heuwelkroon [MS]. Its land is part of Farm 39 [MS]. The municipality's spatial plan says that "although the settlement forms part of Genadendal, it has historically been segregated" [IC]. The plan says Boschmanskloof "was initially established as an extension of the Genadendal Mission" [IC].

The same plan describes "the inter-linked nature of civil services between Greyton, Heuwelkroon and Boschmanskloof" [IC]. It calls for closer links between Boschmanskloof and Greyton, so that the two do not duplicate public facilities [IC]. Boschmanskloof has three seats on the Transformasie Komitee [MS].

So Boschmanskloof's services are tied to its neighbours in Greyton [IC], while its land belongs to the Farm 39 process with Genadendal [MS].

## Madiba Park: on Farm 39, without full services

Madiba Park borders Heuwelkroon [MS]. It stands on Farm 39 land [IC], and the national Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development is the custodian of that land [IC]. People have lived there for more than ten years [TBV]. In 2024 the municipality counted more than 175 structures, and residents said there were more than 220 homes [MS].

In 2024 the municipality told GroundUp that it could not provide full services because "the settlement is not on municipal land" [MS]. GroundUp reported five toilets and some water tanks on the edge of the settlement, kept by the municipality under an agreement with the Komitee, and found one toilet working [TBV]. The full record is on [[civic/residents-association/madiba-park-land-and-services|Madiba Park: Land Status and Basic Services]].

## What happened when people tried to fix things

The record since 2008 shows how many parties even a small fix in Madiba Park involves. Each party is described here only through what it has said or done.

- **2008.** The Komitee obtained a High Court order about Farm 39 land [IC]. GroundUp reported in 2019 that this order stops the municipality from using the land at Madiba Park [TBV].
- **January 2019.** Chemical toilets were removed on 13 January and the water was cut off on 14 January. Residents protested [IC].
- **January 2019.** The municipality said it had "appealed many times to the Department of Rural Development and the GTC to let the residents of Madiba Park stay there temporarily" [MS]. GTC stands for the Genadendal Transformation Committee, the Komitee's name in English.
- **January 2019.** The Komitee's treasurer told GroundUp: "We want [the occupiers] off our land, onto municipal land" [MS]. GroundUp reported that the Komitee would go to court if the municipality put in water or sanitation there, because the land did not belong to the municipality [MS].
- **2022 to 2023.** The municipality budgeted R1.6 million to bring electricity to Madiba Park [IC]. The budget was cut in February 2023, and R73,135 was spent [IC].
- **September 2022.** The DA's Western Cape spokesperson on human settlements said the municipality had written twice to the national Minister asking permission to install interim basic services, and had received no reply [MS].
- **May 2024.** The Komitee's treasurer told GroundUp: "We don't want to evict people, we want to relocate them" [MS]. A municipal spokesperson said the environmental assessment for new housing had caused delays [MS].

In Madiba Park, even a toilet or a water tank has involved the municipality, the Komitee, and the national department [IC]. Each of them has a legal role in that land.

## Before you start something here

- **Check which settlement you are in.** Each has its own page, listed on [[villages/index|Villages]].
- **In Heuwelkroon,** questions about land, housing, roads, and water go to Theewaterskloof Municipality.
- **In Boschmanskloof and Madiba Park,** anything that touches the land goes to the Transformasie Komitee first. That includes building, fencing, food gardens on open ground, water, toilets, and clearing a site. This register sends every entry of this kind to the Komitee for review before it is published.
- **In Madiba Park,** speak with residents before planning anything for them. This register keeps one rule for every page: no proposal about a group of people without that group present.
- **Bring your idea to the Forum.** The [[civic/governance/ward-2-development-forum|Ward 2 Development Forum]] works across the whole valley, and can bring the right people into one room. See [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]].

## What is still open

- Whether the line the Greyton Governance Map draws between Heuwelkroon and Boschmanskloof follows the official boundary between Greyton and Genadendal. A survey in 2005 confirmed the fence between Genadendal and Greyton as the boundary [IC], and this page has not yet compared the two.
- Whether Boschmanskloof lies inside one of Farm 39's township areas. Under TRANCRAA, trust land inside a proclaimed township passes to the municipality [IC], so the answer decides who will own the land under Boschmanskloof's houses.
- Which services the municipality provides in Boschmanskloof today, beyond the links its spatial plan describes.
- Whether Madiba Park households will be housed on Erf 595 in Heuwelkroon.
- The accounts of residents of all three settlements, in their own words.
- The accounts of the Transformasie Komitee and Theewaterskloof Municipality, which have not yet been asked to review this page.
