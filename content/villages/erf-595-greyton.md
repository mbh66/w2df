Erf 595 is a large property around [[villages/greyton|Greyton]] and [[villages/heuwelkroon|Heuwelkroon]]. The Surveyor-General records it as the Remainder of Erf 595 in the Greyton township, made up of seven separate parcels with a combined area of 1,404.89 hectares [IC]. A small part in Heuwelkroon is authorised for 333 homes [IC]. Most of the erf has no published use, and who holds and manages it outside the housing site has not been established [TBV].

**Map:** see this property on the Forum's [Google Earth map of the valley's land](https://earth.google.com/earth/d/1PejJJkRHA_WrwYwdKrSeea92u3q3vp9y?usp=sharing), which shows the Surveyor-General boundaries of all eight properties.

## History

- **Before Greyton.** The land around the Gobos River was Hessequa grazing land, and was granted as the farm Weltevreden in 1793 [IC].
- **1846 to 1854.** [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] bought Weltevreden in 1846 and laid out Greyton, with the first plots sold in 1854 [IC]. Plot owners shared grazing on the commonage around the village [MS].
- **The commonage.** Greyton's histories say Vigne left the land around the village to the plot owners as a commonage, and that it passed to Theewaterskloof Municipality in 2000 [TBV]. No will, deed or title condition has been found [TBV]. *Inference:* the Remainder of an erf in the Greyton township, surrounding the village in several parcels, fits the description of a village commonage. Whether Erf 595 is that commonage, in whole or in part, has not been confirmed.
- **1968 to 1974.** Coloured families forced out of Greyton under the Group Areas Act were moved to Heuwelkroon, which the 2022 heritage study says was built around 1970 [TBV]. The housing site on Erf 595 lies in Heuwelkroon [IC].
- **1977.** The Greyton Nature Reserve was proclaimed on the "mountain commonage" above the village [IC].
- **2000.** Greyton became part of Theewaterskloof Municipality [IC].
- **2022.** A national Department tender states that "the Remainder of Erf 1723 (Portion of Erf 595) was purchased by the Department of Rural Development", with its zoning shown as "Undetermined Zone" [TBV]. A second tender covers Erf 1724, also a portion of Erf 595 [TBV]. *Inference:* parts of the original erf have been subdivided off, which is why the Surveyor-General records the Remainder.
- **2022 studies.** The environmental management programme described "approximately 548 residential erven" for "low cost integrated housing" [MS]. The heritage study found "very little mitigation required" [MS]. The housing site was described as degraded, with alien trees and a deeply eroded tributary channel [IC].
- **2024.** Heritage Western Cape approved the urban design on 18 July 2024 [IC]. GroundUp linked the relocation of Madiba Park households to this site [MS].
- **10 July 2025.** The province authorised 333 homes, a mix of GAP and subsidy housing, for "beneficiaries on the municipal housing list for Greyton" [IC]. The authorisation does not name Madiba Park [IC].
- **2025 to 2026.** The municipality's capital programme listed the project, "Greyton Erf 595 (538)(Yield reduced)(393)", with R10 million from the Human Settlement Grant [IC]. The budget "was removed with the February 2026 Adjustment Budget", after R1,204,753 had been spent [IC].
- **2026.** Ward 2's Community Needs List puts the housing backlog, including "Madiba Park and backyard dwellers", first, and says the municipality "must look at the possibility to use erf. 595, Greyton" [IC].

## On the Forum's map

- The seven parcels lie around Greyton, from the mountain slopes north of the village to land south of it [MS].
- The Gobos dam-wall option drawn at the mouth of the kloof above Greyton (G3) falls inside Erf 595 [MS]. It is an option for the idea on [[concepts/community-built-dams-on-the-baviaans-and-gobos|Community-Built Dams on the Baviaans and Gobos]], which the Forum has not agreed.
- The Forum's map point marked "Sewerage Wetlands" falls inside Erf 595 [MS].
- *Inference:* outside the 6 to 7 hectare housing site, more than 99% of the erf has no use recorded in any source found.

## What is still open

- Who holds title to the Remainder of Erf 595, and which body manages it.
- Whether it is the commonage Vigne is said to have left, and on what terms.
- How each part is zoned and used, and its condition (invasive plants, erosion, fire).
- How much of it lies inside the Greyton Nature Reserve, or is Greyton Shale Fynbos.
- Who the housing beneficiaries are, why the budget was removed, and when building will start.
- What the municipality earns from the erf, and what holding it costs.
- What the date on the Surveyor-General record (22 February 2024) marks.
