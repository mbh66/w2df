Greyton Farm Animal Sanctuary is on Tabularasa Farm, a 40-hectare private farm on Krige Road, about 7 kilometres outside Greyton [MS]. It opened in November 2014 and is a registered non-profit and public benefit organisation (NPO 187-044, PBO 930070994) [MS]. About 200 animals live there: pigs, sheep, goats, cows, geese, chickens, peacocks, a duck, and old or disabled dogs and cats [MS]. The farm runs on solar power, off the grid [MS].

In April 2026 the Global Federation of Animal Sanctuaries gave the sanctuary accredited status [MS]. The sanctuary is funded mainly by its founder, and by donations and animal sponsorships from supporters [MS].

## Needs
- Money to finish its barn. The barn will hold a humane education centre, a resource centre for farmers changing from animals to crops, space for events and workshops, a treatment room for animals, and housing for volunteers. The goal is R1,000,000, and about a third had been raised when the page was last updated [MS].
- More humane educators. One part-time educator now teaches in local schools, and the sanctuary wants at least three full-time educators [MS].
- Sponsors for individual animals, from R50 a month for a chicken to R350 a month for a pig or cow [MS]
- Volunteers aged 15 or older, ideally for a month or more. Accommodation is free, and volunteers pay for their own food [MS]
- Items from its wish list, and donations [MS]

## Offers
- **Sanctuary.** A permanent home for rescued farm animals [MS]
- **Humane education.** Lessons that teach children to care for animals and each other. A programme started in 2015 reached all six schools in the valley, about 2,500 children in Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Greyton, with funding from Humane Society International [MS].
- **Pigs to Plants.** Backyard pig farmers can give up their pigs in exchange for help to turn their space into a vegetable garden, with money, materials, and staff. A team of gardeners recruited locally works on five gardens in the Greyton and Genadendal valley, with more planned [MS]. Produce is sold under the brand The Graceful Garden [MS]. The programme is funded by the Olsen Animal Trust and Humane Society International, and the University of the Western Cape will help measure its results [MS].
- **Visits.** Tours of the sanctuary by appointment [MS]
