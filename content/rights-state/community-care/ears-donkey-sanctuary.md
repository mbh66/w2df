EARS (Equine Animal Rescue Sanctuary) is a registered non-profit (NPO 147-280) that rescues abused and abandoned donkeys and horses [MS]. It started in 2010 [MS]. The sanctuary is on a farm on Middelplaas Road, about 10 kilometres outside Greyton, where more than a dozen donkeys and two ponies live permanently [MS]. It also treats horses that need medical care [MS]. It helps law enforcement officers remove donkeys from abuse [MS].

## Needs
- Donations for the care of the animals [MS]
- Donkey adopters. Supporters can adopt a donkey for R100 a month or R1,200 a year, and receive a certificate and a newsletter every three months [MS].
- Volunteers, including for the charity shop [MS]
- Donated goods for the charity shop [IC]

## Offers
- Rescue, rehabilitation, and a permanent home for donkeys and ponies [MS]
- Visits for school groups, to teach children about caring for donkeys [MS]
- Education for people who own donkeys and horses, and a gelding programme [MS]
- A charity shop on Main Road, Greyton, selling affordable clothes, bedding, and household goods [IC]
