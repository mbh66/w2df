The rules the valley lives by, and what every person is owed. Guiding principle: equality.

## What belongs here

Work about the rules the valley lives by and what every person is owed: the Forum and its agreements, residents bodies, municipal services, land tenure, and care for people who need it.

A farm's production belongs under [[economic-life/index|Economic Life]], even when the land it farms is under discussion here. A school or a heritage project belongs under [[spiritual-cultural-life/index|Spiritual-Cultural Life]].
