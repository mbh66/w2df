> [!quote] The Ward 2 Development Forum (W2DF or simply DevForum) is a community-run forum for Ward 2 of Theewaterskloof Municipality, which covers Genadendal, Greyton, Heuwelkroon, Madiba Park, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, Boschmanskloof and the farms around them. It brings residents, businesses, farmers, schools, conservation groups, and government together every two months to work on five priorities the ward has set for itself. It keeps a public register of Offers and Needs, so that those who can help can find those who need help. The municipality provides administrative support, and the DevForum's members decide its direction.

Anyone may quote the paragraph above to describe the DevForum.

## What the DevForum does

- **It meets every two months.** The first meeting was on 16 September 2026 at the Hester Dorothea Conference Centre in Genadendal [MS]. A summary of each meeting, with a link to the official minutes, is on [[civic/governance/minutes/index|DevForum Minutes]].
- **It works on five priorities.** 
	- Tourism development, 
	- Education and skills, 
	- Agriculture and food sovereignty, 
	- Conservation and environment, and 
	- Business development and local procurement [MS]. 
	- Each priority will have a champion, to be named at the November 2026 meeting [MS]. See [[priorities/index|DevForum Priorities]].
- **It treats the valley as one area.** The DevForum works for Greyton and Genadendal together, as well as for the smaller settlements around them [MS].
- **It shapes proposals with the people they affect.** At its first meeting, the DevForum agreed that affected communities should be involved from the start of any proposal [MS]. [[civic/governance/why-the-forum-shapes-proposals-together|Why the DevForum Shapes Proposals Together]] explains why.
- **It opens a route to government.** The DevForum invites municipal and provincial officials to its meetings and can take ward matters to the Overberg Business Networking Forum [MS]. Practical planning matters, such as land use, zoning and building plans, go to the municipality's officials, which the DevForum helps administer [MS].

## Who takes part

DevForum meetings are open to people from every settlement and sector in Ward 2 [MS]. The first meeting was convened by Michael Haupt for the DevForum and Stalin Govender of Theewaterskloof Municipality's Economic Development office [MS]. People came from the municipality, the Western Cape Department of Agriculture, the Rotary Club of Greyton, Adventure Hub Greyton, the Genadendal Mission Museum, the [[civic/governance/genadendal-transformasie-komitee|Genadendal Transformasie Komitee]], farmers' associations, and guesthouses [MS].

To be told about the next meeting, see [[contact|Contact]].

## What the DevForum leaves to others

- **Land on Farm 39.** The [[rules/trancraa|TRANCRAA]] process for [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] is left to the bodies already dealing with it [MS]. The Transformasie Komitee is the community's mandated body for these matters [MS], and it takes part in the DevForum as a stakeholder [MS].
- **The ward committee.** Ward 2 has a ward councillor (currently Chris Cloete) and a ward committee under the [[rules/municipal-structures-act|Municipal Structures Act]] [IC]. The DevForum does not replace them. Its priorities can be put to them, as well as to the municipality's [[rules/theewaterskloof-integrated-development-plan|Integrated Development Plan]].
- **What appears on this site.** The municipality supports the DevForum with administration. It does not moderate or approve what appears here.

An earlier forum, the [[civic/governance/ward-2-forum-2016-2019|Ward 2 Forum (2016 to 2019)]], worked in the ward before the DevForum.

## This site

This site is a public register of what is happening in the ward. Each entry shows what an initiative needs and what it offers. Residents can find what is nearby, people running projects can be found, and partners from outside the valley can see who is doing what. For the ward itself, its settlements, and its land and government, see [[about-ward-2|About Ward 2]].

The moderators use AI tools to collate large amounts of material, from submissions and from published sources such as books, court records, and archives, into the format you see here. A moderator checks every page before it goes live, and each page lists its sources under *References*.

## How the site is organised

Every entry belongs to one of nine categories. The categories are grouped into three spheres of life, and each sphere has its own guiding principle:

- [[economic/index|Economic]] (cooperation): how the valley makes, grows, and trades what people need.
- [[civic/index|Civic]] (equality): the rules the valley lives by, and what every person is owed.
- [[cultural/index|Cultural]] (freedom): how the valley learns, remembers, worships, and celebrates.

Two themes run through all nine categories: youth and environment. Tags on each entry let you follow these and other themes across the valley.

To see how these categories line up with the Transformasie Komitee's sectors and with a typical residents' association, see [[civic/governance/how-the-forums-categories-compare|How the DevForum's Categories Compare]].

## How entries are checked

A small team of moderators reads every submission before it goes live. They follow these rules:

- **No proposal about a party without that party present.** If an entry describes an organisation or person, that party gets the chance to respond before the entry is published.
- **Land and tenure.** Entries about [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]], [[civic/residents-association/trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA]], land tenure, or the mission settlement go to the Transformasie Komitee for review.
- **Private details stay private.** Phone numbers, email addresses, and home addresses appear only when the person has agreed to be listed. See the [[data-policy|Data Policy]].
- **Facts are marked.** Each claim on an entry shows where it comes from: 
	- [IC] means confirmed by independent sources or an official record, 
	- [MS] means it comes from the initiative itself, and 
	- [TBV] means it still has to be checked.
