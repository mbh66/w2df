This table lists every initiative in the valley that's given approval to be listed on the site. Use the tabs to see one sphere of life at a time, to browse by place, or to find initiatives that are looking for help. Click a name to open its page.

![[register-entries.base]]

## What the needs mean

| Need | The initiative is asking for |
| --- | --- |
| volunteers | People to give their time |
| skills | Specific know-how, such as a trade, mentoring, or teaching |
| funding | Money: donations, grants, or sponsorship |
| equipment | Goods or materials, such as tools, bikes, or supplies |
| premises | Land, a building, or a venue |
| members | People or businesses to join as members |
| participants | People to take part, such as learners, farmers, stallholders, or makers |
| customers | Buyers, visitors, bookings, or advertisers |
| partners | Organisations to work with |

To add an initiative, see [[how-to-submit|How to Submit]]. To offer help, open the initiative's page and follow its "How to get involved" section.
