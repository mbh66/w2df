Samuel Baatjies is an educator at the [[cultural/sport-heritage-arts-culture/genadendal-mission-museum|Genadendal Mission Museum]] [TBV]. He has also worked there as a tour guide [TBV]. He helped the makers of [[history/the-khoekhoe-saga|The Khoekhoe Saga]], a documentary series on the [[history/tribes/khoekhoe|Khoekhoe]], throughout its making, and gave them access to the museum's archives [MS]. He appears in its fourth episode, on faith and the missions [MS]. He also pointed the moderators to the digital transcriptions of [[history/the-genadendal-diaries|The Genadendal Diaries]], and has helped [[contributors/keith-radmore|Keith Radmore]] with the history of [[villages/greyton|Greyton]] [MS].

## Their work

### At the Genadendal Mission Museum

In October 2016 the Heritage Association of South Africa held its symposium in Genadendal. The programme lists Samuel Baatjies as the Genadendal museum tour guide, leading a guided tour of the museum's highlights on 22 October [TBV].

He now works at the museum as an educator [TBV]. The museum holds one of the main archives of the Moravian Church in South Africa, and runs free school programmes on local history, archaeology, medicinal plants, and San and Khoikhoi history. The [[cultural/sport-heritage-arts-culture/genadendal-mission-museum|museum's page]] describes them.

### The Khoekhoe Saga

*The Khoekhoe Saga* is a documentary series written and directed by Johann Abrahams, first shown on SABC2 in January 2023 [IC]. It follows the Khoekhoe from their origins to the revival of Khoi and San identity today, and includes Genadendal among the mission villages it covers [MS].

Johann Abrahams told the moderators that Samuel Baatjies helped with the whole series and gave its makers access to the museum's archives, and that without this help they could not have made it [MS].

Samuel Baatjies appears in the fourth episode, which looks at Khoekhoe belief and at the mission stations [MS]. Speakers in that episode judge the missions in two ways: some say the missionaries helped destroy Khoekhoe culture, and others say the missions gave people who had lost their land a new way of life and taught them to read and write [MS]. The wiki's page on [[history/the-khoekhoe-saga|The Khoekhoe Saga]] sets out what the episode covers.

### Greyton's history

Samuel Baatjies has helped [[contributors/keith-radmore|Keith Radmore]] piece together the history of Greyton [MS]. The story of Greyton's founding is told on [[history/history-of-the-valley|History of the Valley]].
