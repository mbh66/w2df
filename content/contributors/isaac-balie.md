Isaac Balie is a historian of Genadendal and a former curator and director of the [[cultural/sport-heritage-arts-culture/genadendal-mission-museum|Genadendal Mission Museum]] [IC]. He grew up in Genadendal [MS]. He has written two histories of the village: *Die Geskiedenis van Genadendal, 1738 to 1988*, published in 1988, and *Genadendal, The Valley of Grace: A Long Walk Through the History of the First Mission Station in South Africa*, first published in 2020 [IC]. This wiki cites the second book on more than 20 of its history pages. [[history/genadendal-a-long-walk|Genadendal: A Long Walk]] describes it.

## Their work

### Study

His first book, *Die Geskiedenis van Genadendal, 1738 to 1988*, is a reworked version of the thesis for which he was awarded a Master of Arts with distinction (cum laude) [MS]. The university and the year have not yet been recorded.

### At the Genadendal Mission Museum

His book says a museum first opened at Genadendal in 1963, and that the present museum opened in 1987 in the building of 1838 that housed South Africa's first teachers' training college [MS]. The museum's collection was declared a National Cultural Treasure on 8 March 1991 [MS]. The Genadendal Music Archive calls him the museum's founder, and says he directed it for 50 years [TBV]. Which posts he held, and in which years, has not yet been recorded.

In 1995 President Nelson Mandela renamed his official Cape Town residence, then called Westbrooke, Genadendal [IC]. Mission sources say the name came from a letter by the museum's curator, Isaac Balie, written early that year [MS]. *Die Burger* reported the renaming on 2 February 1995 [TBV]. Mandela visited Genadendal on 10 October 1995 [IC]. In his preface to *The Challenge of Genadendal* (2009), he recalled both the renaming and the visit [IC]. [[history/history-of-the-valley|History of the Valley]] tells this part of the story.

From 2011 to 2012 he led a project to make an inventory of the museum's collections, with an assistant auditor from the South African Heritage Resources Agency (SAHRA) [MS]. He also mentored the museum's archival digitisation and auditing team [MS].

In February 2021 the provincial parliament's Standing Committee on Community Safety, Cultural Affairs and Sport visited the museum. Its report names him as the former museum manager, and records that he led the committee through the museum's buildings [IC].

In September 2021 the Western Cape government handed the remains of Michael Balie back to the community of Genadendal [IC]. Michael Balie was one of the first students at the teachers' training college, which opened in 1838 (see [[history/people/carl-jonas|Carl Jonas]]) [MS]. The provincial statement names Isaac Balie as the museum manager. It says the reburial at the museum was conducted by traditional stakeholders and Isaac Balie according to cultural protocols [IC]. He also unveiled a memorial stone for the teachers trained in 1838 [MS]. He told the *Cape Argus* that Michael Balie and his ten fellow students were the first South Africans to be formally trained as teachers [MS].

## Their publications

- *Die Geskiedenis van Genadendal, 1738 to 1988* (1988). Perskor, Cape Town. 263 pages. A cultural history in Afrikaans of the mission's first 250 years, reworked from his MA thesis. It traces how Genadendal became one of the most orderly communities at the Cape in the 1800s, and what its people gave to Afrikaans culture, including some of the earliest printing in Afrikaans.
- *Genadendal: Its Golden Age, 1806 to 1870* (1992). A study of the mission's years of growth. The wiki has not yet found a library record of this book [TBV].
- *Genadendal, The Valley of Grace: A Long Walk Through the History of the First Mission Station in South Africa* (2020; AuthorHouse edition 2023). AuthorHouse, Bloomington, Indiana. A history of the mission village from 1737 to the present, told partly from his own memories. [[history/genadendal-a-long-walk|Genadendal: A Long Walk]] sets out the events it records, and where its dates differ from other sources.

## Work on this wiki

Isaac Balie has helped the moderators with the history of Genadendal over many years [MS]. The wiki draws on his English history, [[history/genadendal-a-long-walk|Genadendal: A Long Walk]], on more than 20 pages, and checks its dates against other sources. The page on the book lists where they differ.
