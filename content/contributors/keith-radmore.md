Keith Radmore is the president of the [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] [MS]. He is a great-great-grandson of [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]], who laid out the village of [[villages/greyton|Greyton]] in the 1850s [MS]. He keeps the family papers that Herbert Vigne's writing desk held, and a collection of books, documents, articles, and stories on Greyton and the Overberg that he has built up over his adult life [MS]. He is now digitising the papers [MS].

## Their work

### The Vigne family papers

Keith Radmore descends from Herbert Vigne through Herbert's son, Herbert Vigne Jnr, who married Kitty Kroukamp [MS]. Through the marriages of the Vigne and Kroukamp families, he is related to other early Greyton and Overberg families, among them the Paerl, Swart, de Wet, Human, Fourie, de Kock, Groenewald, and Henn families [MS].

Herbert Vigne's writing desk came with him from England on the ship *Athenian* [MS]. Keith Radmore gives the year as 1844. The wiki's page on [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] gives 1842, and the date is still to be settled [TBV]. The desk held documents, photographs, letters, and the family Bible [MS]. Keith Radmore has sorted and filed these papers, and is digitising them together with his own collection on Greyton and the Overberg [MS].

[[contributors/samuel-baatjies|Samuel Baatjies]] of the Genadendal Mission Museum has helped him piece together Greyton's history [MS].

### Greyton Transition Town

When he came to live in Greyton, Keith Radmore became an active board member of Greyton Transition Town [MS]. Residents started it in 2011, in response to rising fuel and food costs [MS]. He says it no longer operates because it could not raise the funding it needed, though some of its work has carried on in other forms [MS]. One of these is [[economic/farmers-land-water/valley-food-gardens|Valley Food Gardens]], which Greyton Transition Town started in 2020 [MS].

### The Rotary Club of Greyton

He joined Rotary in 2017 [MS]. The club now has 17 members [MS]. He is its president [MS]. The club took part in the Forum's [[civic/governance/minutes/2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|inaugural meeting]] in September 2026 [IC]. The club's page lists its eleven projects for 2026/27, which run across the valley [MS].

### The feeding scheme of 2020

The national COVID-19 lockdown began on 27 March 2020 [IC]. Keith Radmore says that within a few days, members of the Red Cross, Rotary, Greyton Transition Town, the [[civic/residents-association/greyton-ratepayers-and-residents-organisation|Greyton Ratepayers and Residents Organisation]], the Dutch Reformed and Anglican churches, and local farmers were feeding 350 families across the valley [MS]. He says residents paid for it, raising more than R1 million locally [MS]. [[history/history-of-the-valley|History of the Valley]] records the scheme.

### Across the valley

He says the projects he has worked on have always been for the whole valley: Genadendal and its three satellite villages, Greyton with Heuwelkroon, and the surrounding farms in Ward 2 [MS]. He names the relationship between the communities of Genadendal and Greyton, and work through the churches to strengthen it, as the most demanding part of that work [MS].

## Work on this wiki

Keith Radmore has worked with Michael Haupt, who convenes the Forum, on the valley's history and community work for about ten years, since long before the Forum first met [MS]. Over that time he has done much of the background research behind that work, and helped coordinate it across the valley [MS].
