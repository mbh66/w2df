Michael Haupt convenes the [[civic/governance/ward-2-development-forum|Ward 2 Development Forum]] [IC]. He built this site, drafted its first pages from published sources, and keeps the Forum's records [MS]. He lives in Greyton, and has worked on community projects across the valley for about ten years [MS].

## Their work

### Before the valley

He worked for more than 40 years in corporate change management, telecommunications, and systems integration, in several countries [MS]. He now works as a strategic adviser, and runs Framer OS, which he describes as an operating system for consciousness-based entrepreneurship [MS]. He is the author of *Crisis Codes* [MS].

### In the valley

He has worked with [[contributors/keith-radmore|Keith Radmore]] on the valley's history and community projects for about ten years [MS]. Before the Forum first met, he convened the Greyton Business Forum in 2013, which was then disbanded in 2015 [MS].

On 16 September 2026 he convened the Forum's first meeting in Genadendal, together with Stalin Govender of Theewaterskloof Municipality's Economic Development department [IC]. The Forum meets every two months [IC].

## Their publications

- *Using TIME for difficult decisions* (27 September 2026). Framer OS, on Substack. An essay on how the Forum can make decisions that affect several communities. [[civic/governance/why-the-forum-shapes-proposals-together|Why the Forum Shapes Proposals Together]] draws on it.

## Work on this wiki

- **The site.** He set up this site in September 2026 [MS]. It is built with Quartz from an Obsidian vault, and its files are kept in a public repository on GitHub [MS]. It is one of four linked sites he runs, with the BioConomy wiki, the BioHubs directory, and the Valley of Grace High School Decision Tool [MS].
- **The first content.** He curated the site's first entries and its history pages from published sources: books, court judgments, government reports, archives, and the work of the researchers named in this section [MS]. Each page lists its sources under *References*.
- **The Genadendal Diaries.** He built the [open repository](https://github.com/mbh66/genadendal-diaries) that holds four of the Genadendal mission diaries as tables and plain text, with new English translations [MS]. [[history/the-genadendal-diaries|The Genadendal Diaries]] describes it.
- **Records and moderation.** He keeps the records collected for this site on the Forum's behalf, and handles requests about them, as the [[data-policy|Data Policy]] sets out [IC]. He is one of the moderators who read submissions before they go live [MS].
