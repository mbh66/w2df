## What you'll find here

This site shows what's happening across Ward 2: the farms, the businesses, the schools, the clinics, the churches, the sports clubs, the care groups, the heritage sites, and the projects people are starting.

Every pin on the map is a real initiative with a real contact. Some have been around a long time, like the Mission Church at Genadendal and the farms that have worked this land for generations. Some are new, like the Adventure Hub in Greyton and the small-scale growers on Farm 39. All of them have found a home in the valley we share.

The site is a public register. You can look things up, add something you know about, or follow a theme like water, youth, or heritage. The Ward 2 Development Forum keeps the site running. A small team of moderators, each named on the About page, reads every submission before it goes live.

The Swahili word <i>ushahidi</i> means "testimony." This is where activities in our valley become visible to everyone else, as a living testimony.

## What you get from using the site

<b>Choice.</b> See what's actually available before you commit your time, your money, or your children's future. Which training courses are available. Which schools offer which subjects. Which farms are hiring. Which food gardens are looking for volunteers.

<b>Security.</b> See who the individual is offering a service before you engage. The care worker's supervisor. The garden's coordinator. The farm's contact. The congregation's minister. Contact details stay private unless the entry-owner has agreed to list them publicly.

## Three ways to use the platform

<b>If you live in the valley</b>
Use the map to find what's nearby. See who runs the food garden two streets away, when the next skills course opens, or where to take a service complaint. When you find something you want to join or support, the contact details are on the entry.

<b>If you run something in the valley</b>
Add your project. A business, a farm, a congregation, a sports club, a service, or a one-person practice all belong here. Other residents will find you. So will the municipality, funders, and partner organizations who want to know who is doing what.

<b>If you are looking in from outside</b>
The valley is a container for five towns and two racial histories with a common future. Whether you're with the municipality, a provincial department, a funder, or a partner organization, this site shows Ward 2 as a whole, with named champions in each area of work.

## The eight coordination areas

Everything on the platform belongs to one of eight areas of valley life, each in three broad classifications:

1. <b>Working the land and looking after it:</b> Farmers, Land & Water · Business & Tourism
2. <b>Living together and looking after each other:</b> Governance · Residents Association · Community Care
3. <b>Learning what matters and passing it on:</b> Education · Sport, Heritage, Arts & Culture · Faith Communities

Two themes run through every area: youth and environment. Look for the tags to see what's happening for young people and for the natural world, wherever it sits on the map.

## Why we built it

Until now, the valley has coordinated through WhatsApp groups, occasional meetings, and word of mouth. Exciting initiatives start and others only hear about it months later. Good work sometimes goes unseen. Good partners cannot find each other.

This site puts that work in one place so anyone can see it. That's a small change in how we do things, but it has a large effect. When the valley is visible to itself, it can plan for itself. And in 2038, Genadendal will mark 300 years since the founding of the first Moravian mission in Southern Africa. The work we do together between now and then is what that anniversary will remember.
