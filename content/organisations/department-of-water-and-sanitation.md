The Department of Water and Sanitation is the national department responsible for water. It owns Theewaterskloof Dam [IC] and runs the scheme that shares its water, west to Cape Town and east down the Riviersonderend past the valley [IC]. It administers the [[rules/national-water-act|National Water Act]] together with the [[organisations/breede-olifants-catchment-management-agency|Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency]] [IC].

## What it does

Under the National Water Act the national government, acting through the department, is the public trustee of the country's water [IC]. The Department of Water Affairs and Forestry, which held these duties earlier, published a strategy for the Western Cape Water Supply System in 2007 that is still cited on this site [TBV].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Theewaterskloof Dam.** It is the largest dam in the Western Cape Water Supply System, on the Riviersonderend near Villiersdorp, and the department owns it [IC].
- **Releases down the river.** Farmers along the Riviersonderend and the [[organisations/overberg-water-board|Overberg Water Board]] are entitled to water released from the dam [IC]. In the 2018 drought the department limited releases to irrigators to 60% of their allocation [IC].
- **The Reserve.** In August 2022 the department published the Reserve for the rivers of the Breede-Gouritz water management area, with figures for the Riviersonderend, the Baviaans, and the Gobos [IC]. The Reserve is the share of water the law keeps for people's basic needs and for the river itself [IC].

## Where it touches this site

- [[rules/national-water-act|National Water Act]], which names the department as the body that administers the Act, with its Western Cape regional office as the place to ask.
- [[concepts/from-water-access-to-water-agency|From Water Access to Water Agency]], an idea not yet agreed, which would ask the department whether water added below the dam could be counted. The department has not been asked.
- [[concepts/ridge-to-river-to-table|Ridge to River to Table]] and [[concepts/restoring-the-river|Restoring the River]], ideas not yet agreed, which name the department among organisations whose views are needed.
