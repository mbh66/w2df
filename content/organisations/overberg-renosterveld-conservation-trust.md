The Overberg Renosterveld Conservation Trust is a registered trust that works to protect the last remnants of lowland renosterveld in the Overberg [MS]. It started in 2012, and brings together landowners, communities, wildlife agencies, and non-profit groups [MS]. It is a registered Public Benefit Organisation (PBO 930039578) [MS]. [[economic/farmers-land-water/wild-restoration|Wild Restoration]] lists it as a partner on clearing invasive plants and protecting biodiversity in renosterveld [MS].

## What it does

Renosterveld is the shrubland that once covered the Overberg's lowlands. The Trust says less than 5% of it remains, mostly in small patches on farms, and fewer than 50 of those patches are larger than 100 hectares [MS].

- **Securing land.** The Trust signs conservation easements with farmers, which put restrictions on the title deed, and sometimes buys land [MS]. By March 2022 it had signed 20 easements protecting about 6,000 hectares [MS].
- **The Haarwegskloof Renosterveld Reserve.** [[organisations/wwf-south-africa|WWF South Africa]] handed the Trust the management of this reserve in 2014 [MS]. The Trust has a research and visitors' centre there [MS].
- **Other work.** Managing and restoring renosterveld, restoring watercourses, a project on the black harrier (a bird of prey), education, and research with universities [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

Wild Restoration names the Trust among its partners, for "effective invasive clearing and biodiversity protection within Renosterveld areas" [MS]. Whether the Trust works on any land in Ward 2 is not known.
