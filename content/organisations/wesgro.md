Wesgro describes itself as "the official tourism, trade and investment promotion agency for Cape Town and the Western Cape" [MS]. It is a provincial public entity set up under the Western Cape Investment and Trade Promotion Agency Amendment Act of 2013 [MS]. Its research on visitors to the Overberg is used in [[concepts/overberg-leopard-corridor|The Overberg Leopard Corridor]] [MS].

## What it does

Wesgro markets the province to visitors and investors, and publishes research on tourism in each district [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Visitors.** Wesgro collected 7,588 survey responses from visitors at Overberg tourism offices in 2024 [MS]. It does not publish these figures for each municipality [MS].
- **Targets.** Wesgro's five-year plan describes the provincial target as doubling tourism by 2035 [MS].
