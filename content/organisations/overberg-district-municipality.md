The Overberg District Municipality is the district municipality for the Overberg, with its headquarters in Bredasdorp [MS]. The district is divided into four local municipalities, one of which is Theewaterskloof, the municipality of Ward 2 [MS]. It runs the Overberg District Fire Services, whose control room number is the fire emergency number on the website of [[civic/residents-association/greyton-volunteer-firefighters|Greyton Volunteer Firefighters]] [IC].

## What it does

The municipality states its mission as rendering "sustainable, client directed services" and being "the preferred Provider of Shared Services within the Overberg" [MS]. Fires in its area are dealt with by the district [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Fire.** The fire emergency number on Greyton Volunteer Firefighters' website is the number of the district's fire control room [IC]. Greyton Volunteer Firefighters began with a borrowed trailer and tank, and later used an old fire truck from the district municipality [IC].
- **Planning.** The district is the level above Theewaterskloof in the [[rules/index|The Rules the Valley Lives By]] table, where no district rule has a page yet.
