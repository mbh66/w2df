The Legal Resources Centre (LRC) calls itself South Africa's largest public interest law centre [MS]. It was founded in 1979 by lawyers opposed to apartheid, and gives free legal services to vulnerable people and marginalised communities, with a focus on land and education rights [MS]. On 26 May 2009 it advised the Steering Committee that parts of the Genadendal land settlement conflicted with TRANCRAA [IC].

## What it does

The LRC has offices in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Makhanda, and became a non-profit company in 2022 [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The LRC's advice of 26 May 2009 is recorded on [[civic/residents-association/trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA and Farm 39: The Land Transfer Process]] [IC].
