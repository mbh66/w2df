Globeleq is an independent power producer that develops and runs power plants in Africa [MS]. In South Africa it owns most of Klipheuwel Wind Farm, about 5 km west of Caledon in Theewaterskloof Municipality, and runs it [MS]. The wind farm says its community programmes serve towns across the Overberg, including Genadendal and Greyton [MS].

## What it does

Klipheuwel Wind Farm covers more than 350 hectares and has nine turbines with a combined capacity of 27 MW [MS]. It was one of the first wind farms built under the national Renewable Energy Independent Power Producer Procurement Programme (REIPPPP), and generates about 86 gigawatt hours a year [MS]. Globeleq acquired the wind farm along with several solar plants in South Africa [IC]. Three community and empowerment trusts, including the Klipheuwel Wind Farm Community Trust, together own 20% of the project company [MS].

Under the REIPPPP, wind farms run socio-economic and enterprise development programmes for nearby communities [MS]. Klipheuwel's have included sponsoring teachers and equipment, a school bursary programme and an enterprise development programme for local businesses [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Named beneficiary communities.** Klipheuwel lists Genadendal and Greyton among the areas that benefit from its enterprise and socio-economic development contributions [MS].
- **Supplier open days.** Klipheuwel and Theewaterskloof Municipality held supplier open days in Caledon and Grabouw in November 2025 [MS]. They hosted them again on 12 and 13 November 2026, in partnership with Elgin Community College. People from the valley who took the October training attended the Caledon day: [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-12-msme-supplier-open-day-caledon|MSME Supplier Open Day, Caledon]] [MS].
- **Contact through Rotary.** The Rotary Club of Greyton, working with the DevForum, has been in conversation with Globeleq about funding for work in the valley since September 2026 [MS].
