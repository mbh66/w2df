The South African Heritage Resources Agency (SAHRA) is the national heritage authority, set up on 1 April 2000 under the [[rules/national-heritage-resources-act|National Heritage Resources Act]] in place of the National Monuments Council [TBV]. In 2000 it changed the status of the [[cultural/sport-heritage-arts-culture/genadendal-mission-museum|Genadendal Mission Museum]] collection and the Church Square monuments in Genadendal from national to provincial [MS].

## What it does

SAHRA manages heritage resources of national significance, while each province's authority manages those of provincial significance [TBV]. It keeps the national online inventory of heritage resources, SAHRIS [TBV]. Its head office is in Cape Town [TBV].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Genadendal.** The Genadendal Moravian Mission Complex was declared a national monument in 1980 [IC], and the museum collection a National Cultural Treasure on 8 March 1991 [IC]. In 2000 SAHRA changed the status of the collection and the Church Square monuments from national to provincial [MS].
- **The museum.** From 2011 to 2012 an assistant auditor from SAHRA worked on an inventory of the museum's collections [MS].
