The Western Cape Education Department (WCED) is responsible for early childhood development and for primary and secondary schooling in the province [MS]. The valley's public schools, [[cultural/education/emil-weder-secondary|Emil Weder Secondary]], [[cultural/education/greyton-primary|Greyton Primary]], [[cultural/education/lr-schmidt-primary|L.R. Schmidt Primary]], and [[cultural/education/berea-primary|Berea Primary]], are no-fee schools in its system [IC]. Emil Weder Secondary is in the Overberg education district [IC].

## What it does

The department manages schools through eight education districts [MS]. It names its aims as better language and maths results, better matric results, and wider access to quality education in poor communities [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The schools.** Emil Weder Secondary had 556 learners in 2026, L.R. Schmidt Primary 391, Berea Primary 274, and Greyton Primary 257 [IC]. All four are in quintiles 1 to 3, so learners pay no school fees [IC].
- **A school of skills.** The [[concepts/valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]] idea draws on a school in Bonnievale whose buildings the department helped pay for [TBV]. Whether the department would open a school of skills in the valley is an open question there.
