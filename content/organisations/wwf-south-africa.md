WWF South Africa is the South African office of the global conservation organisation. In 2013 it started a project to clear invasive plants along the Riviersonderend, with funding from government and private partners, and at the end of 2017 it handed the project to the [[economic/farmers-land-water/zonderend-water-users-association|Zonderend Water Users Association]], which named it Restoring the Riviersonderend [MS].

## What it does

WWF South Africa leads the Breede Catchment Water Stewardship Programme, whose first phase ran in 2013 and 2014 [MS]. The programme works with farmers and towns on water risks, clears invasive plants, and has partners including Woolworths and the catchment agency [MS]. It sat on the steering committee of the Greater Cape Town Water Fund in 2020 [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The Riviersonderend.** WWF South Africa started the clearing project that the [[economic/farmers-land-water/zonderend-water-users-association|Zonderend Water Users Association]] now runs [MS].
- **The Breede catchment.** The stewardship programme led by WWF and [[organisations/woolworths|Woolworths]] reports releasing 200 million litres of water a year back into the catchment [MS].
