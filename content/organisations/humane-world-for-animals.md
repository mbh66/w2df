Humane World for Animals is an animal protection organisation. Humane Society International, the name used on other pages of this site, is now a trademark of Humane World for Animals [MS]. Its South African team funds work in the valley run by the [[civic/community-care/greyton-farm-animal-sanctuary|Greyton Farm Animal Sanctuary]], including [[economic/farmers-land-water/pigs-to-plants|Pigs to Plants]] [IC].

## What it does

Its South African team works on care for street dogs, ending cosmetics testing on animals, farmed animal welfare, human-wildlife conflict, and ethical tourism [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Pigs to Plants.** The programme, developed and led by the Greyton Farm Animal Sanctuary, helps households move from keeping pigs to growing vegetables. Humane World for Animals says it has supported it since 2022, and gives it a yearly grant [IC]. The sanctuary names the [[organisations/olsen-animal-trust|Olsen Animal Trust]] as a second funder [MS].
- **Schools.** A programme started in 2015 reached all six schools in the valley, about 2,500 children in Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Greyton, with funding from Humane Society International [MS].
- **Relief.** It gave the sanctuary a disaster relief grant during the Covid lockdown [MS].
