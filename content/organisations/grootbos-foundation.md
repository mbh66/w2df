The Grootbos Foundation is linked to Grootbos Private Nature Reserve near Gansbaai [MS]. It says its conservation team has been a catalyst for conservation on the Agulhas Plain for two decades [MS], and it is building the Agulhas Green Corridor with the [[organisations/cape-leopard-trust|Cape Leopard Trust]] and the [[organisations/endangered-wildlife-trust|Endangered Wildlife Trust]] [MS].

## What it does

The Foundation works to link isolated patches of conservation land into corridors, with partners including the Walker Bay Fynbos Conservancy, the Nuwejaars Wetland Special Management Area, and Agulhas National Park [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The corridor lies south of the valley. [[concepts/ridge-to-river-to-table|Ridge to River to Table]] looks to it as an example [MS].
