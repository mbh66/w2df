The Greater Overberg Fire Protection Association (goFPA) is the fire protection association for the Overberg, registered under the National Veld and Forest Fire Act as FPA 2013/02 [MS]. It helps land users "predict, prevent, manage and extinguish veldfires" [MS], and says it covers more than 660,000 hectares, about 60% of the district [MS].

## What it does

goFPA helps landowners plan firebreaks and sign agreements with neighbours, trains land users' teams, issues burn permits, and runs Fire Management Units whose members help with controlled burns [MS]. Land users join voluntarily and pay a yearly fee based on the size of their property [MS]. It is a registered public benefit organisation [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The [[rules/national-veld-and-forest-fire-act|National Veld and Forest Fire Act]] page names goFPA as the body for membership, Fire Management Units, firebreaks, and burn permits [MS]. Whether its members include landowners in Ward 2 is not yet known.
