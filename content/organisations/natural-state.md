Natural State is an organisation that builds technology and training for restoring nature at large scale [MS]. Its founder and president is Jonathan Baillie [MS]. Its research centre is at the foot of Mount Kenya, and most of its projects are in East Africa [MS]. [[economic/farmers-land-water/wild-restoration|Wild Restoration]] lists it as its partner on trials of sound monitoring [MS].

## What it does

- **Measuring nature.** It builds monitoring tools, called Kutuma and uKweli, to measure biodiversity, carbon, water, and people's wellbeing [MS].
- **Restoration.** It restores land around Mount Kenya through grazing management, natural regrowth, and tree planting [MS]. It runs rewilding units in the Orange River Karoo Conservation Area, which it places in southern Namibia [MS].
- **Paying for results.** It is developing impact bonds, in which investors are repaid according to measured results. They include a Lion Bond in Tanzania [MS].
- **Training.** Courses in landscape restoration, and a grazing and leadership programme with young herders in Laikipia, Kenya [MS].
- **The Rainforest XPRIZE.** It is one of six teams in the finals of this five-year, $10 million competition [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

Wild Restoration names Natural State among its partners, for "bioacoustic monitoring trials" [MS]. Bioacoustic monitoring uses recorders left in the field to pick up the sounds of animals, so that the species present can be counted over time. Wild Restoration says it records sound at its clearing sites in the Riviersonderend mountains [MS]. Natural State's own website does not mention South Africa [MS].
