CapeNature is the Western Cape's public entity for nature conservation, governed by a board under the Western Cape Biodiversity Act 6 of 2021 [MS]. In the mountains above the valley it manages the Riviersonderend Complex, about 28,580 hectares of protected areas that include Vrolijkheid Nature Reserve [IC]. Its stewardship panel has approved a proposed extension of the Greyton Nature Reserve [MS], and it sits on the steering committee of the [[organisations/greater-cape-town-water-fund|Greater Cape Town Water Fund]] [MS].

## What it does

CapeNature describes itself as the province's chief custodian of the natural environment, working through partnerships towards a conservation economy in the Western Cape [MS]. It is a Schedule 3C public entity, governed by a board [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The reserves.** The Riviersonderend Complex covers about 28,580 hectares [IC]. CapeNature's management plan for the Complex names the klipspringer as a sign that its habitat is connected [IC]. [[economic/farmers-land-water/wild-restoration|Wild Restoration]] says its current clearing focus is the high-altitude wetlands of the Greyton Nature Reserve and nearby CapeNature land [MS].
- **The trails.** CapeNature publishes a map and brochure for the Boesmanskloof trail between McGregor and Greyton [IC]. The Greyton Tourism Bureau gives visitors information on CapeNature permits [MS].
- **The Greyton Nature Reserve.** The [[economic/farmers-land-water/greyton-conservation-society|Greyton Conservation Society]] says CapeNature's stewardship panel has approved a proposed extension of the reserve, and that further steps are needed before it is final [MS].
- **Clearing for water.** CapeNature sat on the steering committee of the [[organisations/greater-cape-town-water-fund|Greater Cape Town Water Fund]] in 2020 [MS]. Wild Restoration lists CapeNature as a partner [MS], and has told the Forum that the water fund supports clearing in the Riviersonderend mountains through a partnership with CapeNature [TBV].
- **The Forum.** The minutes of the Forum's first meeting, on 16 September 2026, give "Elsenburg and CapeNature representatives" the action of sharing information on Elsenburg qualifications, host farms, and dates [MS]. CapeNature is not among the organisations listed as attending [MS].
