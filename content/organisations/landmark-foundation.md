The Landmark Foundation is a conservation organisation that works with stock farmers on wildlife-friendly land management [MS]. It says it catches and collars leopards blamed for livestock losses before releasing them, and pays farmers compensation linked to collared leopards [MS]. The people putting forward [[concepts/overberg-leopard-corridor|The Overberg Leopard Corridor]] say it runs leopard cameras in the Greyton Nature Reserve and the Genadendal mountains [MS].

## What it does

The Foundation's work focuses on reducing conflict between leopards and livestock farming, so that farmers do not kill leopards [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Cameras.** The leopard corridor idea reports the Foundation's cameras in the Greyton Nature Reserve and the Genadendal mountains [MS].
- **A collared leopard.** A landowner has written that a male leopard collared by the Foundation in 2014 was later caught in a trap and shot by a farmer near the Rooihoogte pass [TBV].
