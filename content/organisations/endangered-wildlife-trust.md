The Endangered Wildlife Trust is a South African conservation organisation. With the [[organisations/cape-leopard-trust|Cape Leopard Trust]] and the [[organisations/grootbos-foundation|Grootbos Foundation]] it is building the Agulhas Green Corridor, which joins the Walker Bay Protected Environment to Agulhas National Park and the Nuwejaars area [MS].
