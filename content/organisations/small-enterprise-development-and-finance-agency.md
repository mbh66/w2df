The Small Enterprise Development and Finance Agency (SEDFA) is the national government's agency for supporting and financing small businesses and co-operatives [IC]. Its head office is in Pretoria [MS]. The DevForum deals with its office in Eikestad Mall, Stellenbosch, through the Senior Development Officer for Local Economic Development at [[organisations/theewaterskloof-municipality|Theewaterskloof Municipality]] [MS]. In October 2026 it awarded the contract for the [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-10-13-small-business-entrepreneurship-training|Small Business Entrepreneurship Training]] held in Genadendal [MS].

## What it does

SEDFA began operating on 1 October 2024 [IC]. It was formed by merging three agencies: the Small Enterprise Development Agency (Seda), which gave non-financial support such as mentoring and training; the Small Enterprise Finance Agency (sefa), which lent to small businesses; and the Co-operative Banks Development Agency (CBDA) [IC]. The merger followed the National Small Enterprise Amendment Act, signed on 23 July 2024 [IC]. SEDFA is a state-owned company with the state as its only shareholder [IC].

The Department of Small Business Development says the merger gives small businesses and co-operatives one application process and one branch network for both finance and non-financial support [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Training in Genadendal.** SEDFA awarded the contract for a five-module small business course to Eagle Marketing, a training service provider [MS]. The course ran in October 2026 at the Hester Dorothea Conference Centre in Genadendal, coordinated by the [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] [MS]. About twenty participants received certificates [MS].
- **Supplier open days.** SEDFA was listed among the support bodies at the MSME Supplier Open Days hosted by Theewaterskloof Municipality and Klipheuwel Wind Farm in Caledon and Grabouw on 12 and 13 November 2026 [MS]. People from the valley who took the October training attended the Caledon day: [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-12-msme-supplier-open-day-caledon|MSME Supplier Open Day, Caledon]] [MS].
- **A request from the co-operative group.** At their [[civic/governance/between-meetings/2026-11-05-co-operative-follow-up-meeting|meeting on 5 November 2026]], people who took the training asked for Theewaterskloof Municipality to bring SEDFA to a later meeting to explain how to start a co-operative [MS]. SEDFA has not been asked about this.
