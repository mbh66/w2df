# Places Conversation: Offers and Needs

As of 27 October 2026. Prepared by Michael Haupt.

## Context

Nine people met in Stellenbosch on 27 October 2026, convened by Johan Olivier of [[ranyaka-community-transformation|Ranyaka]], to discuss his concept note "A Third Way of Investing in Places" (V1, 28 September 2026). The note names Stellenbosch as the first demonstration place, with Stanford and Genadendal/Greyton as the other two under consideration. Everyone present confirmed a desire to work toward place-based economies, and all have agreed to visit the Valley of Grace as a potential prototype.

Read the concept note: [[a-third-way-of-investing-in-places-v1.pdf|A Third Way of Investing in Places (V1, 28 September 2026, PDF)]].

The note proposes that the place becomes the integration platform. A thin shared professional and digital layer connects local actors to resources that already exist. Success is measured by the "R100 Resource Journey": how much of each R100 committed reaches local actors, stays as local value, and makes the next R100 easier to use. The note tracks seven forms of local capital: human, organizational, economic, social, physical, knowledge and natural.

## Offers and needs

These are best guesses from public roles and the concept note. Each person should correct their own entry.

### Johan Olivier, Ranyaka

#### Offers
- The Third Way model, including the R100 Resource Journey and the seven-capitals measurement frame
- Ranyaka's delivery programs: Proud of My Town, Fix Your Space and the CoCreate Hub
- Funder relationships, including Nedbank through the proposed Stellenbosch Opportunity Network
- Community coordination platform currently in development
- Thirteen years of place-based practice in several provinces

#### Needs
- Demonstration places with an organized local partner
- Funders willing to test pooled, place-based investment
- Independent academic partners to test the cost and localization claims
- Evidence that the model travels beyond the early prototypes in Stellenbosch

### Qhamani Tshazi, Ranyaka

#### Offers
- Research design, policy analysis and stakeholder engagement
- Planning and environmental geography skills for building place intelligence

#### Needs
- Access to local data and community knowledge in candidate places
- Workable methods for measuring natural capital and local value retention
- Research partners to share fieldwork

### Stephan May, Remgro

#### Offers
- An insider's perspective of how corporate social investment and enterprise development decisions get made
- A possible route to Remgro capital, or to procurement in its portfolio companies\
- A possible link to the Rupert Foundation, which is already active in the valley
- The Stellenbosch business network

#### Needs
- Investable, well-governed vehicles with clear reporting
- Evidence that place-based investment lowers total system cost and risk
- Credible local partners who can absorb investment

### Sinazo Nkwelo, DG Murray Trust

#### Offers
- Philanthropic funding experience and possible DGMT support
- A portfolio of place-based work across several municipalities for comparison
- Expertise in collaborative governance between communities and municipalities
- Convening reach through DGMT's Perspectives series and networks

#### Needs
- Places where community-municipal collaboration is working, to learn from
- Evidence that feeds DGMT's place-based strategy
- Co-funders to share risk

### Dr Danie du Plessis, Stellenbosch University

#### Offers
- Spatial analysis and modeling through CRUISE
- Postgraduate students and supervised research
- Expertise in planning systems and spatial transformation
- For the valley specifically, assistance in planning [[ridge-to-river-to-table|Water Retention]] as an investable asset

### Needs
- Field sites with real data and willing communities
- Research questions with practical uptake
- Partners who can co-fund or co-host research

### Prof Sara Grobbelaar, Stellenbosch University

#### Offers
- Frameworks for innovation systems and inclusive development
- Evaluation design and systems analysis
- Engineering students, research projects and the CREST network

#### Needs
- Live cases of inclusive innovation to study
- Data on whether shared technology lowers transaction costs in practice
- Partners willing to co-design and run tests

### Vanessa von der Heyde, The Sustainability Institute

Vanessa wasn't present in the meeting, but was on the invite email group. The following has been prepared based only on publicly available information.

#### Offers
- Long experience running Lynedoch as a place-based learning village
- A working model for a community school and bursary program
- Lessons on what sustains place-based work over many years

#### Needs
- Sustained funding for the school and bursaries, which rely on donations
- Peers to share learning with
- Ways to extend the Lynedoch model to other places

### Leigh Berg, Grootbos Foundation

#### Offers
- Operational experience running community and conservation programs
- Knowledge of the Overberg and its organizations
- The Grootbos model linking hospitality, conservation and community

#### Needs
- An Overberg peer network
- Shared operational infrastructure of the kind the note proposes
- Funding pathways for Foundation programs

### Laura Bergh

#### Offers
- Deep experience enabling a network of social change organizations
- Skill in convening and connecting people across organizations
- An independent voice, free of any institutional mandate

#### Needs
- Time and space during her sabbatical
- When she is ready, work that fits her values
- Low-pressure invitations to stay connected

### Michael Haupt, Ward 2 Development Forum

#### Offers
- W2DF as an organized local partner, with a municipal counterpart in Theewaterskloof Economic Development
- Place intelligence already in progress: the W2DF register, the BioHubs directory and the [BioConomy wiki](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth)
- A Valley of Grace investment case in development
- Decades of systems integration and change management experience

#### Needs
- Funding for coordination and for a demonstration in the valley
- Academic partners for a baseline of the valley's capitals
- A model that honors the community protocol: proposals are developed with the community and never brought to it for endorsement after the fact

## Where offers meet needs

- Johan needs a second demonstration place with an organized partner.
- Danie, Sara and Qhamani need field sites and data. The Valley of Grace needs a baseline of its seven capitals.
- Sinazo's collaborative governance work and the W2DF relationship with the municipality can each inform the other.
- Vanessa's school experience speaks directly to the [[w2df/concepts/valley-of-grace-learning-campus|Valley of Grace Learning Campus]].
- Stephan needs evidence on cost and risk. A Stellenbosch and valley comparison using the R100 Resource Journey could supply it.
