The Western Cape Department of Economic Development and Tourism (DEDAT) is the provincial department for the economy and tourism. It runs the Tourism Growth Fund, which has R7 million for 2026/27 [IC]. A proposed application to the fund for a heritage trail linking Greyton and the mission settlements is on this site as the [[concepts/tourism-growth-fund-application|Valley of Grace Heritage Trail]] [MS].

## What it does

The Tourism Growth Fund gives co-funded grants for tourism infrastructure, new or improved tourism products, and business development, and prefers projects outside the metro and in underserved areas [MS]. Municipalities, tourism organisations, and registered tourism businesses may apply if they are tax compliant and have operated for at least two years [MS]. There is no closing date: applications are assessed as they arrive until the money runs out [MS]. The fund aims to help double international arrivals to the province by 2030 [IC].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The heritage trail.** The proposed application asks the fund for R691,350 of a project costing about R1.8 million [MS]. At the Forum's first meeting, a proposed application of about R1.4 million was presented [MS].
- **Transport permits.** The first meeting agreed that the municipality's representative would raise delays with tourism transport permits at the next DEDAT session [MS].
- **Tour guides.** The provincial registrar of tourist guides sits in this department [TBV].
