The South African National Biodiversity Institute (SANBI) works under the National Environmental Management: Biodiversity Act of 2004 to research and champion South Africa's biodiversity, and runs the national botanical gardens, including Kirstenbosch [MS]. It sat on the steering committee of the [[organisations/greater-cape-town-water-fund|Greater Cape Town Water Fund]] in 2020 [MS].

## What it does

SANBI must manage the national botanical gardens and undertake and promote research on indigenous biodiversity [MS]. Its head office is in Pretoria [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The water fund.** SANBI is a member of the steering committee of the water fund that clears invasive plants in the catchments of Cape Town's dams [MS].
- **The Greyton Nature Reserve.** Brian Rycroft, director of Kirstenbosch from 1953 to 1983, established the Greyton Nature Reserve in 1977 [MS]. See [[history/people/brian-rycroft|Brian Rycroft]].
