The Premier of the Western Cape is the head of the province's government [IC]. The Premier chairs the provincial Cabinet, called the Provincial Executive, and appoints its members [IC]. The office has been held by Alan Winde of the Democratic Alliance since 22 May 2019 [IC]. The Premier's link to the valley runs mainly through [[organisations/theewaterskloof-municipality|Theewaterskloof Municipality]]: the Cabinet the Premier chairs decided the intervention in the municipality in November 2024 [IC]. The Premier's government is described on its own page, [[organisations/western-cape-government|Western Cape Government]].

## What the office does

- **Leads the province.** Under the [[rules/constitution|Constitution]], the executive authority of a province is vested in its Premier, who exercises it together with the other members of the Executive Council [IC].
- **Appoints the Cabinet.** The Premier appoints the members of the Executive Council, gives them their powers and portfolios, and may dismiss them [IC]. In the Western Cape these members are called provincial ministers [IC].
- **Is elected by the provincial parliament.** The Western Cape Provincial Parliament elects the Premier from among its members at its first sitting after an election [IC]. Winde was re-elected this way on 13 June 2024 [IC]. A Premier may serve no more than two terms [TBV].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The intervention in Theewaterskloof.** The Provincial Executive, which the Premier chairs, approved the intervention in the municipality on 6 November 2024 [IC]. It adopts each progress report on the [[rules/theewaterskloof-financial-recovery-plan|Theewaterskloof Financial Recovery Plan]] [IC]. In February 2024, before the intervention, the Premier said he would ask for a full investigation into service delivery in four municipalities, Theewaterskloof among them [TBV].
- **Joint planning.** In June 2019 the Premier's Coordinating Forum endorsed the Joint District and Metro Approach, through which provincial and municipal officials plan together in each district, the Overberg among them [IC].
- **Water.** In the State of the Province Address on 25 February 2026, the Premier gave a target of an extra 310 million cubic metres of water a year by 2035 [MS]. In August 2026 the Premier's office said removing invasive plants from waterways is a key part of the province's water strategy [MS].
- **Farm 39.** The [[rules/trancraa|Transformation of Certain Rural Areas Act]] lets the national [[organisations/minister-of-land-reform-and-rural-development|Minister of Land Reform and Rural Development]] delegate powers in writing to a Premier, who may pass them on to a provincial minister or official [IC]. Whether this has been done for [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] is not known [TBV].
