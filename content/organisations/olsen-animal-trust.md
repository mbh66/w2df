The Olsen Animal Trust is a charitable trust that funds animal welfare work. It is registered with the Charity Commission for England and Wales [TBV]. The [[civic/community-care/greyton-farm-animal-sanctuary|Greyton Farm Animal Sanctuary]] names it, with Humane Society International, as a funder of [[economic/farmers-land-water/pigs-to-plants|Pigs to Plants]] [MS].
