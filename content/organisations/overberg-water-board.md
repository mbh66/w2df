The Overberg Water Board is the only source of bulk drinking water for Caledon, Protem, and Klipdale, and supplies drinking and stock water to farmers in the Rûens [IC]. It is entitled to water released from Theewaterskloof Dam down the Riviersonderend, the river the valley's rivers join [IC].

## What it does

A water board supplies water in bulk to municipalities and other users in its area [TBV]. The Department of Water and Sanitation describes the Overberg Water Board as the sole source of bulk potable water for Caledon, Protem, and Klipdale [IC].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Releases from the dam.** Some of the water the [[organisations/department-of-water-and-sanitation|Department of Water and Sanitation]] releases down the Riviersonderend is for the Board to take [IC]. In the 2018 drought, releases to irrigators were limited to 60% of their allocation [IC].
- **The valley.** The sources found so far do not say where the Board takes its water from the river.
