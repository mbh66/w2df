Rotary International is the worldwide network of Rotary service clubs. The [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] is a service club in Rotary District 9350, part of Rotary International [MS]. The district covers Angola, Namibia, the Northern Cape, and the Western Cape [MS].

## What it does

Each Rotary district is led by a district governor elected for one year [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The Rotary Club of Greyton runs community projects across the valley, from food forests and gardens to early childhood development and a skills centre [MS]. It attended the Forum's first meeting [MS].
