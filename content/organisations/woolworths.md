Woolworths is a South African retailer. It is a partner in the Breede Catchment Water Stewardship Programme led by [[organisations/wwf-south-africa|WWF South Africa]] [MS]. The programme reports releasing 200 million litres of water a year back into the Breede catchment [MS], which takes in the Riviersonderend [IC].

## What it does

The stewardship programme works with farmers who supply retailers, clears invasive plants, and tackles water risks in the upper Breede catchment [MS].
