The Cape Leopard Trust is a conservation organisation that monitors leopards, runs a citizen-science portal for landowners, and teaches in schools [MS]. From May to September 2025 it surveyed the Boland mountains, west of the valley, and identified 38 leopards [MS]. That survey ran from Bainskloof to the Kogelberg coast and did not cover the valley's own mountains [MS].

## What it does

The 2025 Boland survey covered about 2,500 square kilometres with 90 pairs of camera traps, and recorded leopards at 72% of the stations [MS]. It was the Trust's third large Boland survey, after 2010 to 2012 and 2020 [MS]. During it, the Trust gave 42 lessons at seven schools to more than 1,800 learners [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **The valley's mountains.** Leopard has been recorded on trail cameras at Wild Restoration's clearing sites in the Riviersonderend mountains [MS], but no survey like the Boland one has covered them.
- **Corridors.** To the south, the Trust, the [[organisations/endangered-wildlife-trust|Endangered Wildlife Trust]], and the [[organisations/grootbos-foundation|Grootbos Foundation]] are building the Agulhas Green Corridor [MS].
