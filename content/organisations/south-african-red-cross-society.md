The South African Red Cross Society is a member of the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies [MS]. It has a branch in Greyton, the [[civic/community-care/greyton-red-cross|Greyton Red Cross]], at 11 Pointer Street [MS], and its Western Cape provincial office is in Wynberg, Cape Town [MS].

## What it does

The Society lists programmes in community and youth development, restoring family links, health and care, disaster management and climate change, and first aid and health and safety training [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The [[civic/community-care/greyton-red-cross|Greyton Red Cross]] is the Society's branch in the valley [MS]. The [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] lists the South African Red Cross as a partner [MS].
