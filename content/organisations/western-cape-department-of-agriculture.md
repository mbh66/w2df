The Western Cape Department of Agriculture is the provincial department for farming. It runs the Elsenburg Agricultural Training Institute [MS] and funds support for small and land reform farmers through the Comprehensive Agricultural Support Programme (CASP) [IC]. People from the department, including Landcare, attended the Forum's first meeting on 16 September 2026 [MS].

## What it does

Elsenburg offers a B.Agric degree, a Diploma in Agriculture, and Equine Studies, as well as occupational certificate programmes and short courses [MS]. CASP pays for equipment and inputs for farming and food processing, for subsistence, smallholder, and land reform producers and agri-processors in the Western Cape [IC].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **At the Forum.** At the first meeting the department described the Elsenburg qualifications at NQF levels 3 and 4, which it funds, and said applicants must find a host farm for the practical part [MS]. The meeting gave "Elsenburg and CapeNature representatives" the action of sharing information on qualifications, applications, host farms, and dates before the next meeting [MS].
- **Host farms.** The [[economic/farmers-land-water/index|Farmers, Land & Water]] category lists host-farm openings for Elsenburg learners among the things it holds.
- **Co-operatives.** [[concepts/starting-a-co-operative|Starting a Co-operative in the Valley]] names the department's Overberg office as the government office a farming group asks first, and Elsenburg as a possible training partner.
