FoodForward SA is one of South Africa's largest food redistribution non-profits [MS]. It recovers good-quality surplus food from farmers, manufacturers, and retailers and passes it to organisations that serve vulnerable people [MS]. The [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]]'s food security project works alongside it [MS].

## What it does

In 2023 FoodForward SA said the organisations it supplies had grown from 500 to 2,750 in four years, reaching about 985,000 people [MS].

## How its work reaches the valley

The [[civic/community-care/rotary-club-of-greyton|Rotary Club of Greyton]] is bringing fallow gardens back into use, with surplus food going to soup kitchens, old-age homes, and schools, alongside FoodForward SA [MS].
