The Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency manages water use and licensing in the Breede catchment [MS]. It was formed under the [[rules/national-water-act|National Water Act]] by extending the boundary and area of the Breede-Gouritz Catchment Management Agency [IC]. The Riviersonderend, which the valley's rivers join, flows into the Breede [IC].

## What it does

A catchment management agency carries out parts of the National Water Act for one water management area [IC]. The national notice establishing this agency extended the area of the Breede-Gouritz agency [IC].

## How its work reaches the valley

- **Water use and licences.** The [[rules/national-water-act|National Water Act]] page names the agency as the body for water use registration and licences in the Breede catchment [IC].
- **Earlier work.** Its predecessor, the Breede-Gouritz Catchment Management Agency, was a partner in the Breede Catchment Water Stewardship Programme led by [[organisations/wwf-south-africa|WWF South Africa]] [MS].

## Where it touches this site
