Cullinan & Associates Inc is a firm of attorneys in Cape Town that specialises in environmental law [MS]. It began in 1997 as Winstanley Attorneys, and took its present name after it was incorporated [MS]. It describes itself as the oldest specialist environmental law firm in South Africa [MS]. One of its directors, Cormac Cullinan, wrote *Wild Law: A Manifesto for Earth Justice* (2002), and led the drafting of the Universal Declaration of the Rights of Mother Earth [MS].

## What it does

- **Environmental law.** The firm lists about seventeen areas of practice. Among them are water law, conservation, heritage, environmental impact assessment, agriculture and fisheries, mining and energy, climate and pollution, and the rights of nature [MS]. It says it takes on "high-impact, precedent-setting cases that protect our constitutional rights, our people and the environment" [MS].
- **Cannabis and natural medicines.** In 2021 it opened a branch for cannabis and natural medicines law [MS]. A partner organisation reports that one of its attorneys acted for the amaMpondo people to end the aerial spraying of cannabis crops with glyphosate in the Umzimvubu region of the Eastern Cape [MS].

## Its director and the rights of nature

The rights of nature is the idea that rivers, mountains, forests and other parts of nature can hold rights in law, as people and companies do. Cormac Cullinan's book *Wild Law* (2002) was the first to use the term "Earth jurisprudence" for this field [IC]. In 2010 a Peoples' World Congress on Climate Change in Bolivia adopted the Universal Declaration of the Rights of Mother Earth, which he helped draft [IC]. In the same year he co-founded the Global Alliance for the Rights of Nature [IC]. He sits as a judge on the International Rights of Nature Tribunal [MS], and is chief executive of EnAct International, an environmental governance consultancy he founded in London [MS].

In April 2025 Daily Maverick reported that Cullinan is the driving figure behind the Table Mountain Rights initiative, which seeks to have Table Mountain (Hoerikwaggo) recognised as a legal person [TBV]. Of the mountain he said: "Officially, it's just a pile of rock, a national park. What's obvious to me is that there is this disjuncture between our experiential reality and our educated worldview" [MS]. The article does not say whether the firm takes part in the initiative.

## How its work reaches the valley

No page on this site yet records work by the firm in Ward 2. A copy of the 2012 trust deed of Die Bevryde Grond Trust held by the Forum bears the firm's stamp, and Cormac Cullinan helped draw it up [MS]. The idea [[concepts/bevryde-grond-trust|Bevryde Grond Trust]] asks whether a trust on the same lines could help resolve the Farm 39 deadlock under TRANCRAA. The firm has not been asked about it.
