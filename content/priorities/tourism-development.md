Growing tourism across the whole valley: heritage, nature, and conservation tourism, events, trained guides, and a working visitor information service.

The Forum agreed that tourism across the whole valley is its first priority. The meeting raised heritage and nature tourism, weddings and events in Genadendal, the need for trained tour guides, the lack of a working tourism office in the ward, and delays with tourism transport permits. See the [[2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|minutes of the first meeting]].

## Champion

A champion for this priority will be named at the Forum's November 2026 meeting.
