The Forum agreed five priorities at its [[2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|first meeting]]. Each will have a champion, to be named at the November 2026 meeting, when the order of the priorities will also be confirmed. Work on a priority can sit in more than one category. Each priority has its own page, which lists the initiatives working on it.
