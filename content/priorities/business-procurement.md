Keeping more money in the ward: stronger links between small businesses, established businesses, farmers, tourism operators, and support agencies, and local supply chains.

The Forum agreed that business development and local procurement form part of its programme. The aim is to build local supply chains, so that more of the money spent in the ward stays in the ward. See the [[2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|minutes of the first meeting]].

## Champion

A champion for this priority will be named at the Forum's November 2026 meeting.
