Caring for the valley's land, water, and wildlife: clearing invasive plants, restoring indigenous vegetation, and a possible Overberg leopard corridor.

The Forum agreed that conservation and environment should be a priority of their own, and not only part of tourism. The meeting discussed clearing invasive plants, restoring indigenous vegetation, and a possible Overberg leopard corridor. See the [[2026-09-16-inaugural-meeting|minutes of the first meeting]].
