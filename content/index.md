This site lists the initiatives in Ward 2 of Theewaterskloof Municipality: Genadendal, Greyton, Heuwelkroon, Madiba Park, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Boschmanskloof. Each entry shows what an initiative needs and what it offers, so residents, Forum champions, and outside partners can find each other.

New here? Start with [[getting-started|Getting Started]]. To read the site in another language, see [[reading-in-your-language|Reading this wiki in your language]].

## Where the valley is heading

- [[long-term-goal|Our Long-Term Goal]]: what the Forum is working towards by 2038, and how a yearly survey will show whether life in the valley is changing.
- [[future-of-the-valley|Future of the Valley]]: why 2038 matters, and how the ideas the valley is working on now could shape the next 300 years. Not yet agreed.
- [[concepts/index|Ideas for the Valley]]: big ideas the valley could work towards together. None is agreed yet. Each is tabled at a Forum meeting so that everyone it affects can have a say.

- [[history/index|Valley History]]: the full story of the valley, and pages on the peoples, institutions and historical figures who shaped it.

## Follow a theme

Two themes run through every area: youth and environment. Other themes include water, heritage, tourism, women, land tenure (TRANCRAA), and the 2038 tercentenary. Use the tags on any entry to see related work across the valley.
