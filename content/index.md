This site belongs to the people of Ward 2, a valley below the Riviersonderend mountains in the Overberg, Western Cape. Seven settlements share the valley with the farms around them: Genadendal, Greyton, Heuwelkroon, Madiba Park, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Boschmanskloof.

The DevForum (or Ward 2 Development Forum, W2DF) is where the people of these villages meet to plan together. This site is the DevForum's public record. It shows what's happening in the valley and what each project offers and needs.

## Why the DevForum exists

For a long time the valley has worked together through WhatsApp groups, occasional meetings, and word of mouth. A good project might start in one village, and everyone else hears about it months later. People who could help each other never meet.

The DevForum first met on 16 September 2026 to change that. Residents, farmers, businesses, schools, churches, conservation groups, and government now sit at one table every two months. They've agreed on five things to work on first: tourism, education and skills, farming and food, the environment, and local business. See [[priorities/index|Priorities]].

This site lets the whole valley see what's happening in one place. When the valley can see itself, it can plan for itself.

In 2038, Genadendal will mark 300 years since the first Moravian mission in Southern Africa began there. The DevForum is working towards that date. See [[long-term-goal|Our Long-Term Goal]].

## Browse by area of valley life

Every project on the site sits in one of nine areas of valley life. The nine are grouped into three spheres.

![[w2df-spheres-and-categories.png|The W2DF logo at the centre, surrounded by the three spheres of life (Cultural, Economic and Civic) and their nine categories, with youth and environment as an outer ring|600]]

- **[[economic/index|Economic]]**: how the valley makes, grows and trades what people need. 
	- See [[economic/farmers-land-water/index|Farmers, Land & Water]], [[economic/business/index|Business]] and [[economic/tourism/index|Tourism]].
- **[[civic/index|Civic]]**: the rules the valley lives by, and what every person is owed. 
	- See [[civic/governance/index|Governance]], [[civic/residents-association/index|Residents Association]] and [[civic/community-care/index|Community Care]].
- **[[cultural/index|Cultural]]**: how the valley learns, remembers, worships and celebrates. 
	- See [[cultural/education/index|Education]], [[cultural/sport-heritage-arts-culture/index|Sport, Heritage, Arts & Culture]] and [[cultural/faith-communities/index|Faith Communities]].

Youth and environment run through all nine areas. The tags on each page let you follow these and other themes, such as water, heritage and the 2038 tercentenary, across the valley.

## Can I trust what I read here?

A moderator reads every page before it goes live. If a page describes a person or an organisation, they get a chance to respond first. Facts on most pages end with a short tag in square brackets that shows where they come from:

- **[IC]** confirmed by an official record or by independent sources (Independently Corroborated).
- **[MS]** comes from the project or organisation itself (Mission Sourced).
- **[TBV]** still to be checked (To Be Verified).

[[about|About the DevForum]] explains how pages are checked. The [[data-policy|Data Policy]] explains how personal information is kept, and [[contributors/index|Research Contributors]] names the people whose knowledge helps write the site.
