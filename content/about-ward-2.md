> [!quote] Ward 2 is one of the fourteen wards of Theewaterskloof Municipality in the Overberg, Western Cape. It covers seven settlements and the farms around them, in the valley below the Riviersonderend mountains: Genadendal, Greyton, Heuwelkroon, Madiba Park, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, and Boschmanskloof. Genadendal began as a Moravian mission in 1738, and Greyton as a village laid out on a private farm in 1854. The ward's people share one valley, one ward councillor and one municipal plan, while their land is held in different ways.

## The settlements

- **[[villages/genadendal|Genadendal]].** Founded in 1738 by the Moravian missionary [[history/people/georg-schmidt|Georg Schmidt]], and the oldest mission village in South Africa [IC]. The mission buildings around Church Square form a Provincial Heritage Site [IC].
- **[[villages/greyton|Greyton]].** Laid out by [[history/people/herbert-vigne|Herbert Vigne]] on the farm Weltevreden, with the first plots sold in 1854 [IC]. It became a municipality in 1910 [IC].
- **[[villages/heuwelkroon|Heuwelkroon]].** Part of Greyton, on its edge. Under the Group Areas Act, coloured families were forced out of Greyton, and most were moved to Heuwelkroon [IC].
- **[[villages/madiba-park|Madiba Park]].** A settlement of its own, bordering Heuwelkroon and standing on Farm 39 land [MS]. The municipality does not provide basic services there [MS].
- **[[villages/bereaville|Bereaville]]** and **[[villages/voorstekraal|Voorstekraal]].** Two villages linked to the Genadendal mission, about 10 kilometres from Greyton [MS]. Their children go to [[cultural/education/berea-primary|Berea Primary School]], started by the missionaries in 1865 [MS].
- **[[villages/boschmanskloof|Boschmanskloof]].** A small community near Greyton, with a history that Greyton sources trace to the Hessequa leader [[history/people/stoffel-koekson|Stoffel Koekson]] in the late 1600s [TBV].

See [[villages/index|Villages]] for every settlement and the initiatives in each.

## The land

How land is held is the biggest difference between the settlements.

- **Farm 39.** [[villages/farm-39|Farm 39]] is the legal name for the land on which Genadendal, Bereaville, Voorstekraal, Boschmanskloof and Madiba Park stand [MS]. In 1858 it was granted to the [[history/institutions/moravian-church|Moravian Church]] in trust for the people living at the mission [MS]. Since the Rural Areas Act of 1987 the state has held most of it in trust for residents [IC]. The [[rules/trancraa|Transformation of Certain Rural Areas Act]] (TRANCRAA) of 1998 provides for the land to pass to a body the residents help choose, and that transfer has not yet taken place [MS]. The [[civic/governance/genadendal-transformasie-komitee|Genadendal Transformasie Komitee]] is the community's mandated body for this process [MS]. Its record is on [[civic/residents-association/trancraa-process-farm-39|TRANCRAA and Farm 39]].
- **Greyton.** Greyton's plots were sold with title deeds from 1854, and residents own them as private property [TBV].
- **The mountains.** Most of the mountain land above the valley is held by CapeNature [MS]. The [[economic/farmers-land-water/greyton-nature-reserve|Greyton Nature Reserve]], about 2,200 hectares, was proclaimed on the mountain commonage in 1977 [IC].

Eight properties in the Surveyor-General's records surround the settlements, covering about 11,194 hectares [MS]. Each has its own page under *The land* on [[villages/index|Villages]].

## How the ward is governed

- **The ward councillor and ward committee.** Ward 2 elects a councillor to the Theewaterskloof council. The councillor chairs a ward committee of up to ten other people, which can make recommendations to the council on any matter affecting the ward [IC]. See [[rules/municipal-structures-act|Municipal Structures Act]]. The current Ward 2 Councillor is Chris Cloete.
- **The municipality's plan.** Each year the municipality reviews its [[rules/theewaterskloof-integrated-development-plan|Integrated Development Plan]] (IDP), and the budget must follow it [IC]. For the 2026 review it held one public meeting for Ward 2, on 27 November 2025 in the Moravian Hall in Greyton [IC]. The plan records thirteen needs for Ward 2, starting with the housing backlog [IC].
- **The municipality's finances.** Since November 2024 the municipality has been under an intervention by the Western Cape Government, and since October 2025 it has worked under a binding [[rules/theewaterskloof-financial-recovery-plan|Financial Recovery Plan]] [IC].
- **Bodies in the valley.** The Transformasie Komitee deals with Farm 39 land. The [[civic/residents-association/greyton-ratepayers-and-residents-organisation|Greyton Ratepayers and Residents Organisation]] speaks for its members in Greyton [MS]. The [[about|Ward 2 Development Forum]] brings the whole ward together around five priorities [MS].

The laws, by-laws and plans that apply in Ward 2 are on [[rules/index|The Rules the Valley Lives By]].

## The people

Official figures treat the seven settlements as one ward, so the differences between them do not show [IC]. The latest counts for single settlements are from Census 2011: Genadendal had 5,663 people and Greyton 2,780 [TBV]. Figures for the municipality as a whole are on [[challenges|Challenges: The Record and the Present State]].

The [[concepts/the-yearly-valley-survey|Yearly Valley Survey]] is an idea for giving the valley its own figures, settlement by settlement. It is not yet agreed.

## Where the history begins

Before 1652, the Chainoqua and the Hessequa were the main Khoekhoe peoples of the Overberg, and some Chainoqua kraals stood near where Genadendal is today [IC]. Georg Schmidt founded the mission in 1738, and its school, begun in 1793, still runs today as [[cultural/education/lr-schmidt-primary|L.R. Schmidt Primary]] [MS]. South Africa's first teachers' training college opened at Genadendal in 1838 [IC]. In December 2000 the valley became part of Theewaterskloof Municipality [IC].

In 2038 Genadendal will mark 300 years since the mission was founded. The full story is on [[history/index|Valley History]].
